## 文案

Japan calls it Subaru. The Māori call it Matariki. ✨
Across the Pacific it’s almost one word: Matariki, Matariʻi, Makaliʻi. Linguists trace it to mata-liki, "little eyes"; Māori lore says the eyes of a god.
Romania sees a hen with her chicks, Siberia a duck’s nest, Japan a bundle. And three places (China, Babylon, the Bugis of Indonesia) all saw a tuft of hair.
This month it’s up all night: look east after dark.
Next Wednesday: the White Tiger, Orion in a Chinese sky.
