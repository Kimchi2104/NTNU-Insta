## 文案

China: Shen (“three”). Egypt: Sah. The Kamilaroi: Birray Birray. ✨
Three similarly bright stars in a straight line, almost on the celestial equator — from the Arctic to New Zealand, they rise due east.
And three peoples saw fire here. For the Kamilaroi of Australia, the Belt is three young boys, Rigel their campfire and the Sword their fire stick. For the Maya, three stars form a triangle of hearthstones — creation’s fire — and the Orion Nebula is its smoke. For the Aztecs, it was a wooden fire drill: every 52 years all fires were put out and a new one was drilled on a hilltop.
Early January, around 20:00 (mid-northern latitudes): look southeast. Red Betelgeuse upper left, blue-white Rigel lower right, the Belt between — and below it, Sirius.
