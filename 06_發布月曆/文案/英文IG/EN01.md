## 文案

An island under 0.4 km² — and a sky full of sailing stars. 🐦
On Anuta, the biggest thing in the sky is a bird: Sirius is its body, Canopus and Procyon its wings. The north wing is shorter — it was broken in a fight with the demigod Motikitiki over the goddess Taro, the star Antares.
5,600 km west, the Bugis of Sulawesi see the same three stars as a chicken: Manuʼ. Same stars, same word.
