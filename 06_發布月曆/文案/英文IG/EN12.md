## 文案

Ostriches, gazelles, hyenas — a desert zoo overhead. 🐪
In old Arabic star names, the sky is full of animals. The biggest is a lion: legend says its tail struck the ground and the gazelles leapt — three pairs of stars below the Great Bear are the hoofprints, Alula, Tania and Talitha.
The biggest family is the ostriches: Fomalhaut and Diphda are two males, with chicks between them, mothers in Cetus and a nest with eggs in Eridanus. The star name Azha comes from the Arabic for “ostrich nest,” with one letter miscopied.
Arabian ostriches died out in the 20th century. In the sky, they remain.
