## 文案

An umbrella, an egg, or no shell at all? 🌌
For about 800 years, ancient China argued over the shape of the sky.
☂️ Gaitian: the sky is an open canopy over a square earth — and the sun is carried round like an ant on a millstone.
🥚 Huntian: the sky is like an egg, the earth its yolk. It could be built as an instrument — and became the mainstream.
🌫️ Xuanye: the sky has no substance. Sun, moon and stars float in empty space. It lost — but it came closest to today’s universe.
