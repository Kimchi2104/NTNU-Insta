## 文案

One sky: a dragon, a moose hunt, a fishing net. ✨
China saw a dragon, a Forbidden City and a white tiger. Mongolians tied the sky to a golden stake; an Evenki hunter chases a moose every night. Hawaiʻi, Tonga and Anuta found their way home by the stars. The Blackfoot painted the Big Dipper and the Pleiades on tipi tops; Belarusian farmers saw Elijah’s fiery chariot. The Kamilaroi and Euahlayi link the Milky Way’s dark lanes, not just the stars. And the same Big Dipper is a ship to the Bugis, a fishing net to the Mandar.
Some saw a palace, some a fishing net, some the way home — yet almost every culture looked up and wrote its days on the sky.
In the northern sky tonight: after dark, look east for the Summer Triangle. Vega and Altair are the Weaver and the Cowherd; their night, Qixi, falls on 8 August.
Thank you for looking up with us. Next Wednesday: the Azure Dragon — why China saw a dragon.
