## 文案

Meet once a year? Light alone takes 14.6 years to cross. 🌌
Tonight is Qixi. The Cowherd is Altair — legend says the two small stars beside him are his carrying pole with their two children. The Weaver is Vega — in the Northern Hemisphere, the brightest star nearly overhead on August evenings. Between them, in dark skies: the Milky Way.
Add Deneb and you have the Summer Triangle. The magpie bridge is often said to be Deneb, in Tianjin, “the Heavenly Ford”.
Happy Qixi!
