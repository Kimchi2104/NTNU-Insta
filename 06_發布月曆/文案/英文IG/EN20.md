## 文案

A compass with no needle — just stars. 🧭
For Bugis sailors of South Sulawesi, key stars rise or set over known islands. Alpha Centauri and Hadar are the Widow: her groom died before the wedding. Seen from Balobaloang at dusk in late July, she marks the way to the port of Bima, and crews set course by her. Beside her, the Southern Cross is a lame house — one post too short.
North of the equator? Late March, after dark, look northeast: the Big Dipper, handle down. To the Bugis, that’s the ship.
