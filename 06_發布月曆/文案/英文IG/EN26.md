## 文案

Who drew the lines in your star app? Not the IAU (the International Astronomical Union). ✏️
In the 1920s the IAU defined 88 constellations, but only their borders. The lines inside were never ruled on, so the same lion is 10 lines in Stellarium, 18 for H. A. Rey (who drew Curious George), and 32 for Alexandre Ruelle in 1786.
Ruelle, a “student astronomer” at the Paris Observatory, took the mythical pictures off his star map and kept only straight lines. Too early: it didn’t catch on.
Stars are astronomy. Lines are culture. Grab a sheet of paper and draw your own.
