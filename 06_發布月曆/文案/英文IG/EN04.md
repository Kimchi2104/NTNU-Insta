## 文案

In the Arabian desert, people watched the Pleiades to know when the rains would come. 🌙
In the desert, the first star seen at dawn marked the season: 28 stars in a ring, 13 days each (one lasts 14) — 365 days, beginning when the Pleiades return in June.
Mid-October brings the rains, al-Wasm, “the brand.” On December 7 the Scorpion’s head rises at dawn as the Pleiades set, and the forty coldest days begin.
This month, look east after dark: the Pleiades are already up. “When the Pleiades rise at nightfall, the shepherd looks for a cloak.”
