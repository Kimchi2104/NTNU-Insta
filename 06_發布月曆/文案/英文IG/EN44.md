## 文案

Four beasts guard China’s sky. Who sits in the middle? 🦌
The Four Symbols guard east, south, west and north — but the centre is empty. An old Chinese list of five creature chiefs fills it: phoenix, qilin, sacred tortoise, jiaolong dragon… and, for the creatures with no feathers or scales, the sage: a human.
Which stars belong to that human? Our club lists three candidates: Xuanyuan (the Yellow Emperor’s name), Vega and Altair, and Hushi, the Bow and Arrow, aimed at Sirius. No surviving text says. Still no answer.
Early January, mid-northern latitudes: after dusk Vega and Altair sink low in the west; Sirius rises in the east-southeast around 19:00–20:00; Regulus, in Xuanyuan, rises in the east around 21:00.
Next Wednesday: Orion — three stars the whole world knows.
