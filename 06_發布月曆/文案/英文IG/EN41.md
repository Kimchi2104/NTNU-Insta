## 文案

In Japan, Orion’s Belt is a plough. 🌾
Japan copied China’s 28 star mansions, but not the names. Edo-period scholars gave them Japanese readings: a rod for levelling rice, possibly a bellows, a plough. Most are scholars’ readings, not folk names, and 11 of the 28 still have no agreed meaning.
Two are really old. Subaru, the Pleiades, one of Japan’s oldest star names, means “gathered into one bundle”; a dictionary recorded it around 934 CE, and in 1955 a carmaker took the name. Karasukiboshi is the plough in Orion’s Belt, and the Bugis, Romanians, Belarusians, Siberians and Tukano also see farm tools or workers there.
