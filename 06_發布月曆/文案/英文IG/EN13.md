## 文案

A black bird’s egg began one dynasty. A red bird, the next. 🐦‍🔥
The Vermilion Bird of the south is built from seven lunar mansions; its middle third is Chunhuo, “Quail Fire.”
In legend, the Shang descended from an egg — dropped by a dark bird, a swallow in the old commentaries, not the Vermilion Bird. The red bird came with the Zhou: the Guoyu says Jupiter stood in Quail Fire when King Wu attacked, the Zhou’s own sky.
This Lunar New Year, Jupiter is back in Quail Fire: look east after dark for the brightest light.
Happy Lunar New Year! Next, on 17 Feb: the Sámi reindeer hunt.
