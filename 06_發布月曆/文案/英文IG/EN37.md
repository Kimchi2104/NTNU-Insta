## 文案

At the center of the sky: the emperor’s palace. 🏯
In the Chinese sky, everything turns around Ziwei, the palace of the Sky Emperor — the Forbidden City is named after it. Inside: the five Pole stars, one of them called Di, “the Emperor.”
Twist: today’s Pole Star was not his star. 3,000 years ago he lived in Kochab. Earth’s axis wobbles; the Emperor moved.
Outside the palace: a government (Leo’s tail, Denebola, is an emperor’s seat) and a market. Even the Sky Emperor goes shopping.
Early May, 21:30, northern sky: all three cities are up.
Next Wednesday: an emu made of darkness.
