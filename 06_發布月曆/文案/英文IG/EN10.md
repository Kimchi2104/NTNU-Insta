## 文案

Painted on top of the tipi: the Big Dipper and the Pleiades. ⛺
On the Great Plains, the Blackfoot (Niitsitapi) painted the top band of some tipis black: a night sky with the Big Dipper, the Pleiades and a cross for the Morning Star. The designs came in dreams and belong to their owners.
The Pleiades are the Lost Children: six poor children with no yellow calf robes, mocked by the others, who got lost on the prairie and were taken into the sky. So in spring, while the calves are yellow, they are gone; when the calves turn brown, they come back.
From northern latitudes, look high in the south around 20:00 this month.
