## 文案

In Tonga, some star names are sailing orders. ⛵✨
From Tonga the Pole Star never rises — from the main island, it stays more than 20° below the horizon. So the way north uses other stars.
Kapakau ʻo Tafahi, Tafahi’s Wing: like a hen with wings spread, brooding over the island of Tafahi, 313 km north of Vavaʻu — too far to see the island, only the stars.
ʻAo ʻo ʻUvea, ʻUvea’s circlet: a ring of small stars. Sail by it only when it sits over ʻUvea like a headband.
Which stars? Sources differ. Stellarium guesses Cassiopeia and Corona Borealis.
