## 文案

A bull’s leg, a cart, a bear, a bier, a gang of robbers — all the Big Dipper. 🥄
① Ancient Egypt: Meskhetyu, a bull’s foreleg, drawn on coffin lids.
② From northern and eastern Europe to China: a cart.
③ Homer: the Bear, the only one that never bathes in the ocean.
④ Seven sages (India), seven old men (Mongolia), seven brothers and their little sister (Blackfoot).
⑤ Arabia: a bier, carried by its daughters.
⑥ Macedonia: robbers circling all night, like thieves around a house.
⑦ The Tukano of the Amazon: a serpent that swallows the river’s fish.
⑧⑨ A ship for the Bugis, a fishing net for the Mandar.
⑩ The Evenki of Siberia: a moose and its hunter.
Tonight, in the Northern Hemisphere: high in the northeast after dark. Can you see al-Suhā, the faint star beside Mizar?
Next Wednesday: follow the handle’s arc to a star.
