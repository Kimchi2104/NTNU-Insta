## 文案

Follow the Dipper’s handle: arc to Arcturus. ✨
Find the Big Dipper (in April it rides high in the northeast after dark). Follow the curve of its handle in a big arc, about three fists at arm’s length, to an orange star: Arcturus, the brightest star in the northern sky. Extend the arc south about as far: blue-white Spica.
The arc is real: Mizar, Alkaid, Arcturus and Spica lie almost on one circle, and Stellarium has the line built in. The Greeks named Arcturus Arktouros, the guardian of the bear.
Mid-April, around 21:00 (Northern Hemisphere): Arcturus in the east, Spica lower in the southeast.
