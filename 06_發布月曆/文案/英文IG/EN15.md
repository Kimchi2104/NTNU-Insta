## 文案

The god honoured at the Lantern Festival shares a name with the centre of the sky. 🏮
The Lantern Festival is the Upper Yuan — called the Heaven Official’s birthday in folk tradition: “the Heaven Official grants blessings.” He is also called Ziwei Dadi. Ziwei is also the name of the Ziwei Enclosure, the star region around the north pole: the Sky Emperor’s palace. Same name, two different things.
Just outside its walls, Sima Qian said, the Big Dipper is the Sky Emperor’s chariot.
Tonight after dark, look east: a nearly full Moon with Jupiter and Mars close by.
This Wednesday: Belarus, where the same Dipper is Elijah’s chariot.

## 首留言

Sources: Shiji, “Treatise on the Heavenly Offices”: “the Dipper is the Emperor’s chariot”; Book of Jin, “Treatise on Astronomy”: the Ziwei wall of fifteen stars, “seat of the Great Emperor”; Scholars of Daoism mostly treat the Heaven Official and the Ziwei Emperor of the Four Sovereigns as two different gods, often mixed up because both carry “Ziwei”.
