## 文案

Same Big Dipper: the Bugis saw a ship, the Mandar a fishing net. 🎣
The Mandar live on Sulawesi’s west coast, facing the Makassar Strait: fishers and traders. Their Bugis neighbours see the Big Dipper as a ship, and even call two of its bowl stars “the Mandar Widow”.
The Mandar put those two stars on a boat that runs through Draco to Boötes. The rest of the Dipper: three fishermen in the middle, fishing gear at each end. Together: Bittoéng Panjala, the fishing-net star. Old fishermen say that when they see it late at night in April, the cool season is near — and a fish called bambang appears.
Almost every recorded Mandar star pattern belongs to the sea. The researchers note they only asked coastal fishermen; no one has asked inland rice farmers yet.
