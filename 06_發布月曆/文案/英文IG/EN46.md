## 文案

Not one animal, but two: a turtle and a snake. 🐢🐍
China’s guardian of the north, Xuanwu, rules winter, water and death. Its stars: seven lunar mansions that arc across the south on September evenings, from Dou to Shi and Bi — the Room and the Wall, which the West calls the Great Square of Pegasus.
Just north lies the snake: Tengshe, the Flying Serpent. To the south camps a sky army, with Fomalhaut — Beiluo Shimen — at its gate.
Next Wednesday: Mid-Autumn, and where the Moon rests.
