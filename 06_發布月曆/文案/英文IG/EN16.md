## 文案

In Belarus, the Big Dipper is a cart — driven by the prophet Elijah. ⚡

Into the 21st century, researchers found village elders in Belarus who still knew the old star names.

⭐ The Nail and the Cart
The Pole Star is Hvozdz, the Nail: the whole sky turns around it. Around the Nail goes Voz, the Cart — the Big Dipper. The Little Dipper is Maly voz, the Small Cart.

⚡ Then the cart got a driver
After Christianity arrived, the Cart became Illioŭ voz, Elijah’s cart. The Bible says “a chariot of fire” came between him and his companion, “and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven” (2 Kings 2:11). In East Slavic folklore, summer thunder is Elijah driving; his feast day is in early August — thunderstorms and harvest time. Some think the driver was once the Slavic thunder god Perun.

🌾 A farmer’s sky
Orion’s Belt is Kastsy, the mowers: three stars in a row, like men mowing a field. Below them is a rake. Boötes is a ploughman, the Pleiades are a flock of hens, Capella is a she-goat, and the Milky Way is the Goose Road.

🛒 Not just Belarus
The Norse saw the Man’s Chariot, Romania the Big Cart, China the Sky Emperor’s chariot.

🔭 This week
Later in the evening, look northeast: the Cart is climbing the sky.
