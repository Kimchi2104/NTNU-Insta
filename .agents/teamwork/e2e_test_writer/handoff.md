## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Ephemeris Precision Derivation**:
   - Astronomical claims in scripts (e.g., `A-13` Canopus max altitude in Taipei = 12.3°, Kaohsiung = 14.7°; `H-七夕` Vega-Altair distance ~15 ly, separation ~34.2°) were tested against Skyfield 1.54 using topocentric coordinates and local DE421 ephemeris.
   - Skyfield yielded 12.27° for Taipei and 14.68° for Kaohsiung, exactly confirming the values in `A-13:68`.
   - Spherical trigonometry and law of cosines yielded angular separation $\theta = 34.20^\circ$ and 3D spatial distance $D = 14.63\text{ ly}$, validating the Qixi astronomical assertions.
   - For `A-01`, Spica rises at 19:47:06 CST on the 2026 spring equinox, reaching an altitude of 2.29° at 20:00 CST in the East-Southeast (azimuth 103.6°), which validates the ancient "龍抬頭" observation window while accounting for 2,500 years of precession shift.

2. **Parsing & Tokenization Logic**:
   - Naive ASCII word counters split accented foreign proper nouns into invalid fragments (e.g. `Al-Thurayyā` into `Al`, `Thurayy`, `a`).
   - The harness implements a Unicode-aware regular expression `\b[\w\u02bb\u02bc]+(?:[-\x27\u2019][\w\u02bb\u02bc]+)*\b` that preserves Arabic macrons, Inuktitut orthography, and Polynesian okina (`ʻ`).
   - For Chinese voice-overs, punctuation marks denote pauses rather than spoken syllables; character counting uses ideographs `[\u4e00-\u9fff]` plus alphanumeric tokens `[a-zA-Z0-9]+`.
   - On-screen card text stripping removes quote marks and middle dots (`・`, `·`) to accurately measure reading length against the 14-character limit.

3. **Multi-Column Priority & Schema Verification**:
   - In English shot tables (`| Shot | Sec | Voice-over | On-screen text | Words/sec |`), naive matching for substring `"sec"` erroneously mapped to `words/sec`. The column detector was prioritized so that `words/sec` is matched first, ensuring `col_sec_idx` and `col_wps_idx` are accurately disambiguated.
   - The schema validator enforces all quantitative hard constraints: total duration $\le 180\text{ s}$, shot pace $\le 2.6\text{ words/sec}$, text cards $\le 6\text{ words}$, opening line matching *"One sky."*, and YouTube title $\le 60\text{ characters}$.

4. **Git Immutability & Workspace Synchronization**:
   - The user integrity requirement mandates zero modifications to original scripts in `04_腳本庫/*.md`. `verify_sync.py` uses `git status --porcelain 04_腳本庫/` and verifies that no tracked original files have status `M`, `D`, or `R`.
   - For dual-workspace mirroring, `verify_sync.py` computes SHA-256 checksums across all 55 deliverable files and validates `sha256(worktree) == sha256(downloads)`.
