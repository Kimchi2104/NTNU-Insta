# 任務：萬國星空英文版改寫（一次一集）

> 使用方式：先讀 `02_製作流程SOP/英文版評估.md` 開頭的「團隊決定（11/1）」與第七節的翻譯規範；把下面整段貼給代理，附上該集**已查核**的中文逐字稿（v1 或 v2，不是 v0）。檔期與標題看 `06_發布月曆/節目表_萬國星空_英文版.csv`。

---

You are adapting a Traditional-Chinese script for a short astronomy-culture video series, **World Sky Cultures** (「萬國星空」), made by the National Taiwan Normal University Astronomy Club. Each episode is a 9:16 vertical video: animated star charts plus simple diagrams. **There is no voice-over**: the script is shown as on-screen text over light music. The English edition is built for reach — international viewers who know nothing about the series — and is published on Facebook, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Threads.

**Adapt, don't translate word-for-word.**
- Target length **60–90 seconds** (festival shorts 30–60 s). This is not a translation: pick the **one** story in the Chinese script that a stranger scrolling past would stop for, and tell only that. The Chinese version is about 180 s; expect to keep roughly a third of it.
- Reading speed **≤ 2.5 words per second** per shot (viewers read, they do not listen). One short sentence per screen.
- Reuse the Chinese animation: every English shot must name the Chinese shot (and, if known, the Canva page) it reuses. Avoid concept diagrams that have Chinese text baked into the image, or list them as "needs English version".
- The first 2 seconds must carry the hook (the English schedule gives one). No "last week we…" bridge — international viewers have not seen last week.
- Opening line pattern: *"One sky. The [people] saw — [X]."*
- Chinese star names: pinyin + meaning on first mention (*Xin, "the Heart"*); call Chinese 星官 *asterisms*, not constellations. Korean: Revised Romanization. Japanese: modified Hepburn. Sanskrit: IAST. Arabic and Indigenous names: spell exactly as the Chinese script's 星名表 gives them (with diacritics).
- Sky-watching tips: write them for the Northern Hemisphere mid-latitudes ("this month, after dark, look east"), not for Taipei. Distances in light-years.
- Do not add any claim that is not in the Chinese script. If the Chinese script hedges ("有人認為"), keep the hedge.
- On-screen text cards: ≤ 6 English words each.

**Output** (write to `04_腳本庫/_英文版/{ID}-EN-script-draft.md`):
1. Shot table: `| Shot | Sec | Main text | Headline card | Reuses (Chinese shot / Canva page) | Words/sec |`
2. Total seconds, total words.
3. Glossary table of every name used: `| Chinese | English used | Notes |`
4. YouTube title (≤ 60 characters), description (3–5 lines + sources), and one caption usable on Instagram, Facebook and Threads (first line = the hook; sources in the first comment).
5. A list of places where you cut or changed meaning, so a human reviewer can check them.
