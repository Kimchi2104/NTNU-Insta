# 任務：萬國星空英文版改寫（試點用，一次一集）

> 使用方式：先讀 `02_製作流程SOP/英文版評估.md` 第七節的翻譯規範；把下面整段貼給代理，附上該集**已查核**的中文逐字稿（v1，不是 v0）。

---

You are adapting a Traditional-Chinese script for a short astronomy-culture video series, **World Sky Cultures** (「萬國星空」), made by the National Taiwan Normal University Astronomy Club. Each episode is a 9:16 vertical video: animated star charts plus simple diagrams, with voice-over. The English edition targets international students and astronomy enthusiasts, published on YouTube Shorts and Facebook.

**Adapt, don't translate word-for-word.**
- Target length **≤ 180 seconds**; speech rate **≤ 2.6 words per second** per shot. The Chinese version is usually longer — cut 15–30%, keep the facts that carry the story, drop wordplay that only works in Chinese (or explain it in one short clause).
- Keep the shot structure (one row per shot) so the existing animation can be reused; you may merge or drop shots, and change the seconds per shot.
- Opening line pattern: *"One sky. The [people] saw — [X]."*
- Chinese star names: pinyin + meaning on first mention (*Xin, "the Heart"*); call Chinese 星官 *asterisms*, not constellations. Korean: Revised Romanization. Japanese: modified Hepburn. Sanskrit: IAST. Arabic and Indigenous names: spell exactly as the Chinese script's 星名表 gives them (with diacritics).
- Times: 24-hour clock, "Taiwan time" where relevant. Distances in light-years.
- Do not add any claim that is not in the Chinese script. If the Chinese script hedges ("有人認為"), keep the hedge.
- On-screen text cards: ≤ 6 English words each.

**Output** (write to `04_腳本庫/_英文版/{ID}-EN-script-draft.md`):
1. Shot table: `| Shot | Sec | Voice-over | On-screen text | Words/sec |`
2. Total seconds, total words.
3. Glossary table of every name used: `| Chinese | English used | Notes |`
4. YouTube title (≤ 60 characters), description (3–5 lines + sources), and a Facebook caption.
5. A list of places where you cut or changed meaning, so a human reviewer can check them.
