## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week" bridge; the Islamic lunar calendar (354 days, Ramadan moving); the 13 × 28 + 1 arithmetic in the on-screen text (kept on the diagram; the Caption gives “one lasts 14”); the 1,200-year-old "heat, crumbling grass, wild donkeys" line; the stations after the Pleiades (Follower, Orion's head, Gemini's feet, the lion's forearm) and the lion; Suhail and its two proverbs (Suhail kept only on the diagram); the Shen–Shang comparison with last week; the "same day as 大雪" note; Venus and the al-Hawdān story; the name card; the Moon meeting the Pleiades (qirān) and the 21 December "thirteenth night"; Polaris, the daughters of Naʿsh and the two guards; the Taiwan-time viewing guide (18:30, 05:45 Scorpion's head); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by Monday's English solstice short).

Wording to check:
1. The Caption's first line no longer says "Bedouins": the Chinese says 「沙漠裡的人」/「牧人和農人」 and uses 貝都因 only as a hashtag. The cover keeps the series line `One sky. The desert people saw — a calendar.` The schedule CSV's hook cell (English hook column) still has the old "Bedouins …" wording; not changed here (only the 英文稿 status column is touched).
2. E02 "Desert seasons are hard to read from the ground — so people read the stars." = 02 鏡「沙漠裡的季節，不容易從地上看出來……得抬頭問星星」.
3. E04 "first appears before dawn" = 04 鏡「六月七號前後，al-Thurayyā 在黎明前第一次露臉」.
4. E05 "Mid-October: the rains … al-Wasm, ‘the brand’" = 07 鏡「十月中……雨季開始了：al-Wasm，意思是『烙印』——第一場雨，在乾地上烙下一片綠」. "the four groups of stars behind the lion" dropped.
5. E06 "Legend says" = 07 鏡「傳說」.
6. E07 "December 7" = 08 鏡「十二月七號」 (folk-almanac date; PyEphem in Riyadh gives about Dec 8, kept on the diagram source line only).
7. E09 hedge: 09 鏡「伊斯蘭以前的人相信……昴宿落下的那幾天，被認為雨最多」. "people believed … the Pleiades most of all" keeps the belief framing for both halves; the diagram adds "(Ibn Qutayba, paraphrased)" and that Islam rejected it as superstition (Chinese diagram 引文層).
8. E11 headline "Over 1,000 years ago, Arabs said" = 11 鏡「一千多年前的阿拉伯人說」. "looks for a cloak" = 「要找斗篷了」.
9. E12 is the Northern Hemisphere version of 11 鏡 + 16 鏡 (「太陽一下山，昴宿已經掛在東方」「紅色的畢宿五在它下面」); no clock times or altitudes.
10. E06 "on sand, truffles" compresses 「落在沙地上，會長出松露」 to keep the beat at 2.2 w/s.
11. Diagram C-A13-01 English: the Chinese marks 「今天 12/11」; the English says "Mid-December" (the EN publish date is 12/16, still inside the Crown station 12/7–12/19). The 芒種／處暑／大雪 solar-term notes are dropped from the English diagram.
12. Diagram C-A13-02 English: the last Chinese line (上週參商) is dropped; four lines remain.
13. E13 "Monday, December 21": EN05 (winter solstice) publishes Monday 12/21, so the end card does not say "Next Wednesday".
