## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last week, ten lives of the Dipper” bridge and the ten-figure flash; the Taipei 8 p.m. Dipper position (replaced by a Northern-Hemisphere tip); the Stellarium screenshot; the *Shiji* (Sheti, “the handle leads the Dragon’s horn”) and the Chinese asterisms; the *Heguanzi* handle clock, the Han-dynasty equinox and “three weeks later” (C-X04-02); Hesiod’s dusk rising and the vines (C-X04-03); the Sámi bow and Fávdna, the Tukano serpent’s light, the Arabic armed and unarmed Simāk; Hōkūleʻa, Hikianalia and the two canoes (C-X04-07); Arcturus’ distance, size and proper motion, Halley 1718 (C-X04-04); Spica’s distance, binary and Qianlong-era light; the Spring Triangle and Great Diamond and the Regulus caveat (C-X04-05); the Taipei 9 April viewing card with the crescent Moon, Jupiter and Mars (C-X04-06); Citrā and Svātī and the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode). Only C-X04-01 is used, so only it has an English version (`_概念圖/en/`).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “In April, after dark, it rides high in the northeast” replaces 02 鏡「這幾天晚上八點，它在東北方半天高，斗柄朝右下」 (Taipei). Checked for 14 Apr, 21:00 local time at 40° N: Dubhe ~68° up a little east of north, Alkaid ~53° up in the northeast.
2. E03–E04 “About three fists away” = 「手臂伸直，大約三個拳頭遠」 (“arm outstretched” is cut to keep the narrow box to 3 lines; it stays in the caption and in the hand sketch); “the northern sky’s brightest” = 「北半天球最亮的恆星」 (brightest star north of the celestial equator; the diagram wording).
3. E05 “Carry the same arc south, about as far” = 03 鏡「再順著同一道弧，往南延伸差不多一樣遠」; “blue-white” = 「藍白色的星」.
4. E06 “sit almost on one circle” = 04 鏡「幾乎就在同一個圓上」 (diagram: radius 45.6°; Mizar 0.16° off, Alioth 2.1° off).
5. E07 = 「連天文軟體 Stellarium，都把這條線內建成指星的輔助線」.
6. E08 “the guardian of the bear” = 08 鏡「希臘人叫大角 Arktouros，守熊的人」. “the bear” is not identified with the Dipper on screen (the Chinese does not say so either).
7. E09 is the Northern-Hemisphere tip replacing the Taipei 9 April tip (14 鏡); it is not in the Chinese script. Checked for 14 Apr, 21:00 local time at 40° N: Arcturus ~37° up in the east, Spica ~25° up in the southeast. No altitudes on screen (daylight saving time shifts them by an hour). Jupiter and Mars are left out.
8. E10 follows the English schedule (EN24, 2027-04-21: *India: The Moon’s 27 Wives*).
9. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook (written for this v0: *Follow the Dipper’s handle: arc to Arcturus.*), not a translation of the Chinese cover’s second line.
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 67 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
