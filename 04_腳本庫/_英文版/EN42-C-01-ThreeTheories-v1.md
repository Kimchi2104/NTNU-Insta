## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the names Luoxia Hong and Yixing (kept only as “from the Han dynasty to the Tang”); Zengzi’s “four corners” objection; Gaitian version 2 (“sky like a bamboo hat, earth like an upturned bowl” — survives only as the small diagram label “Sky like a hat” on the three-schools diagram); the side view with 60,000 / 80,000 li; the seven paths and six spaces (七衡六間) and their diameters; the 8-chi gnomon and solstice shadows; “one inch per thousand li” and the Tang survey that disproved it; the pole 36 degrees up, equator, ecliptic, 28 mansions half seen / half hidden; “why the sky looks blue” (青非真色); the comparison card; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “From the Han dynasty to the Tang: about 800 years” = 「從西漢落下閎造渾儀，吵到唐朝一行實地量影子，足足八百年」 (104 BCE → 724 CE ≈ 828 years; “about” added because the Chinese “足足八百年” is a round number).
2. E03 “Three schools” = 《晉書》「古言天者有三家」; umbrella / egg / no shell = 「天是一把傘、一顆蛋，還是根本沒有殼？」.
3. E04 “first version”: the Chinese says Gaitian had two versions and the umbrella-and-chessboard is the early one (the diagram title also says “two versions”). Version 2 is cut, so “first version” keeps it from being read as the only Gaitian.
4. E05–E06: in the Chinese, the ant comes after version 2 (“the sun turns flat around the pole”). The Book of Jin quote with the ant is from the same Zhoubi-school passage as version 1 (首留言), so placing it right after version 1 does not add a claim. “How? An ant on a millstone” = 「怎麼會是平轉？蓋天家說：像螞蟻在磨盤上爬」.
5. E07 “in a text attributed to Zhang Heng” keeps 「傳為張衡所作」.
6. E09 “could be built as an instrument and used for calendars” = 「這顆球能直接做成儀器、算出曆法」.
7. E10 Cai Yong’s two quotes: 「考驗天狀，多所違失」→ “often wrong” (the diagram box adds “Tested:”); 「近得其情」→ “close to the truth”.
8. E11 “Its books are long lost” = 「書早就失傳」. Xi Meng is named only on the diagram.
9. E13 “speed up, slow, turn back” = 「時快時慢、忽進忽退」.
10. E14 “no method handed down” = 「絕無師法」 (literally “no teachers’ method” — no lineage of teaching).
11. E15 keeps the Chinese hedge 「它說的氣當然不是今天的物理」 → “Its qi is not modern physics”; “closer to today’s universe than the other two” = 「比另外兩家都更接近今天的宇宙」.
12. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook, not a translation of the Chinese cover.
13. No sky-watching tip: the Chinese script has none (the alliance post’s Polaris line is not part of the episode).
14. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive: blocks overlap across pages.
