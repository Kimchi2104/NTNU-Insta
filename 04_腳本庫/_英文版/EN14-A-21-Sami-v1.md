## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday’s Vermilion Bird; reindeer herding today; the church’s suppression and the burnt drums (diagram C-A21-01); the 1736–37 French survey of Lapland and Le Monnier’s reindeer constellation (diagram C-A21-02; only the 2025 IAU names are kept); the helpers (Gállá’s sons = Orion’s Belt and the skis legend, the ski runners = Castor and Pollux, the Pleiades as hunting dogs); who Gállá is and Sirius at 4° from Sápmi (C-A21-07); the Milky Way’s two names (bird path, year mark); Arcturus as the spear-bearer in ancient Arabia; the Taipei 12 Feb times (20:00 / 22:01 / 23:00); the three 9:16 cards (One Big Dipper, look up this weekend, Sámi star names); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 = 02 鏡「薩米人住在挪威、瑞典、芬蘭的北部，一直到俄羅斯的科拉半島」.
2. E03 = 03 鏡「留下來的星座不多，卻都在同一個故事裡」.
3. E04 keeps the hedge 「公馴鹿，也有人說是駝鹿」 as “some say a moose”. Stellarium’s word is “elk”, which in American English is a different animal (wapiti), so “moose” is used, as in EN50.
4. E05 “In late 2025” = 「二〇二五年底」 (WGSN 25 Dec 2025); “bull and cow reindeer” = 「公馴鹿、母馴鹿」.
5. E06 “aimed at the reindeer” = 「弓朝著北方的那頭鹿」.
6. E07 “the star that holds up the sky” = 「撐住天空的那顆星」 (Stellarium: Sky Support).
7. E08 keeps “Legend says” = 「傳說」.
8. E09 “the bow is drawn, but the arrow waits. The hunt is not over yet.” = v1’s 「每天晚上都拉滿，卻遲遲沒放——這場狩獵，到今天還沒結束」 (v1 deliberately avoids “never”; so does the English).
9. E10 = 10 鏡「西伯利亞的獵人說是一頭駝鹿，因紐特人說是馴鹿，到了薩米，卻是獵人的弓」. Inuit 馴鹿 → “caribou” (North American reindeer; EN07 glossary).
10. E11 is the Northern-Hemisphere tip replacing the Taipei 12 Feb tip; it is not in the Chinese script. Checked for 17 Feb, 23:00 local time at 40° N: Arcturus ~19° (ENE), Dubhe ~61° (NE), Alkaid lower, Capella ~51° and Mirfak ~34° (NW–W), Caph ~17° (NW), Polaris 40°. The Chinese gives the same arrangement for Taipei at 23:00 (「十一點前後，獵人、弓、北極星和鹿都在天上，北極星在正中間」); the Chinese caveat 「仙后座已經很低」 is a Taipei fact (5–7°) and is dropped because at 40° N Cassiopeia is ~17° up.
11. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook, not a translation of the Chinese cover’s second line.
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
