## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "sky is a day on the island" card; Taro/Antares setting time; the Taki lead stars and α Pic; the south celestial pole; the two Magellanic Clouds; the fishing net / Southern Cross / "Internet" joke; the 1916 conversion; the star path to Tikopia; the *manuk → Basay detail (kept only as "traced to Taiwan"); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 "under 0.4 km²" = Chinese 「不到零點四平方公里」 (0.37 km² measured); "one of Polynesia’s smallest inhabited islands" = Chinese 「玻里尼西亞有人長住的島裡，最小的之一」.
2. E03 "by island tradition" = 「島上的傳說」 (oral tradition, not archaeology).
3. E07 "Sirius is its body, Canopus and Procyon its wings" — Chinese names the wings: Canopus = east wing (Te Kapakau Tonga), Procyon = north wing.
4. E08 angles 26° / 36° = 25.7° / 36.2° in the Chinese (rounded as the Chinese narration does).
5. E09 "the demigod Motikitiki" — the Chinese explains he pulled Anuta up from the sea floor; kept in the diagram caption only.
6. E11 "5,600 km" = 5,586 km computed; "Linguists trace the word to Taiwan: manuk" (no asterisk on screen) compresses 「南島語的祖先說 manuk，北台灣的巴賽語也說 manuk」.
7. E13 "Canopus only 12° up" = Taipei upper culmination 12.3°; "around 02:00" = Chinese 「凌晨兩點朝正南看」 (02:00 Sirius 47°, Procyon 60°; Canopus culminates 02:19). Time given in Taiwan terms on purpose (this is our sky); international viewers get the general idea.
8. The cover's second line is the schedule's hook, not a translation of the Chinese cover's second line.
9. E13 is set as three short lines in Canva ("From Taiwan (25° N) in late / Nov: Sirius and Procyon sit / high, Canopus only 12° up.") so it stays clear of the Canopus label; same words as the table.
10. E09 "the goddess Taro" = Chinese 06 鏡 「為了女神 Taro」 (sources: a female spirit).
11. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 90 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive: blocks overlap across pages.
