## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week's emu" bridge; Gacrux's orange colour and the Coalsack beside the true cross (02 鏡; the Coalsack stays only in E06's "no Coalsack" and as a label); "drop straight down" is kept only as E05's headline; the names of the cross in other cultures (04 鏡: Tonga, Hawaiʻi, Anuta, Mandar, Bugis) and the name card; the Diamond Cross's 10° long axis and why it never completes from Taiwan (07 鏡, kept only as a label); Taiwan in spring, when the False Cross stands due south first (08 鏡) and its card; the Chinese asterisms (09 鏡); Tonga's two ducks (10 鏡); precession and Ptolemy (11 鏡); the Taipei altitudes and the moon (12–13 鏡) and the "tonight" card; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 "Mix them up and you lose south" sums up 01 鏡 + the 17 鏡 teaser of A-31 (「認錯了，你找到的南方會偏掉二十度以上」); no number on this page.
2. E03 "The smallest constellation: four bright stars in a kite, plus a faint fifth" = 02 鏡「全天最小的星座。四顆亮星排成風箏形，十字裡還有第五顆小星」.
3. E04 "Extend the long bar about 4.5 times … within 3°" = 03 鏡「從十字架一往十字架二，延長大約四倍半，就到南天極附近，差不到三度」 (the lines cross 2.95° from the pole).
4. E06 "About 30° west. No bright pointers beside it, no Coalsack." = 05 鏡「再往西大約三十度……它旁邊沒有那兩顆亮晶晶的指針，也沒有煤袋」; "bigger, dimmer" = 「更大、整體暗一點」.
5. E07 "more than 20°" = 06 鏡「離它二十度以上」 (22° and 28° on the chart).
6. E08 headline "South: 24° east or 42° west" = the Chinese 字卡 「偏東 24° 或偏西 42°」; the diagram keeps the hedge (at sea, 21° S, at culmination only).
7. **E10 (derived)**: "From 22° N … 5–11° up" = 12 鏡 Kenting / Lanyu 「離地平線五到十一度」 at 21:00 on 5/14 (Gacrux 10.9°, Acrux 5.0°). "North of about 33° N, it never rises" is the 譯名表 note for the Southern Cross (Gacrux, dec −57.1°, has its highest point on the horizon at about 33° N; Acrux already at about 27° N). EN28 airs 5/19, when the cross is upright about 20 minutes earlier than on 5/14; "around 9 p.m." covers both.
8. E09 "a name from modern stargazers" = 07 鏡「這是現代觀星的人取的名字」; "all four stars in Carina" = 「四顆星都在船底座」.
9. E11 = EN29's working title *Before the Compass: Finding the Way by Stars*.
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 73 s).
