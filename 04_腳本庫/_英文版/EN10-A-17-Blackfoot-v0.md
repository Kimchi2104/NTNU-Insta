## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last week’s Navajo episode; the dusty stars on screen (kept only on the diagram); the seven brothers and their sister (Big Dipper story, 05–07); the Dipper as a night clock, “the last brother” (08); the Pole Star as a hole in the sky (09); Feather Woman and the turnip (10); the Spider Man’s lodge (11); Scarface Poïa = Jupiter (12) and the two morning stars of 1905 (13); the dogs version of the Lost Children (16); the Wolf Trail line (17, kept as a label only); the Taipei tonight-and-tomorrow card with Venus and Jupiter (18); the Egypt bridge (19, replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “Today: Alberta and Montana” = 02 鏡「今天分住在加拿大的亞伯達和美國的蒙大拿」.
2. E03 “In a big summer camp, a writer counted: one in ten was painted” = 03 鏡「他們夏季的大營地裡，十頂帳篷就有一頂畫了圖」 (McClintock: 35 of 350 at one Sun Dance camp; written as one camp to answer the Chinese 待查核 #7 about over-generalising). The Chinese 「一百多年前」 is dropped.
3. E05 “The designs came in dreams and belong to the owner. No one else may copy them.” = 04 鏡「這些圖案屬於主人，是夢裡得來的，別人不能照著畫」.
4. E06 “after the buffalo hunt” = 14 鏡「春天獵到野牛」.
5. E07 “mangy old bulls” = 「長癬的老公牛」 (McClintock).
6. E08 “were taken up into the sky” = 15 鏡「被帶上了天」 (passive kept; the Chinese does not say by whom).
7. E09/E10 “Gone while calves are yellow … In autumn, when the calves turn brown, they come back every night” = 15 鏡「春天，小牛還是黃的時候，天上看不到他們；等到秋天小牛轉成褐色，他們才每晚回來」. The diagram’s dates (May 1, Jun 24, Oct 10) are the Chinese diagram’s Browning computation.
8. E11 is rewritten for the Northern Hemisphere: the Chinese says the Pleiades are overhead at 20:00 in Taipei on 15 January. On 20 January they cross the meridian around 19:50 local time, about 66–76° up between 50° N and 40° N, so “ride high in the south” holds.
9. Cover second line = the schedule’s hook (it says “on top of the tipi”, as the Chinese note corrected from “at the door”).
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 80 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
