## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday (Ibn Mājid) and the earlier episodes (Mid-Autumn, the Arabian desert calendar); the fist-and-a-bit scale and diagram C-A32-01; Qurʾān 10:5 and Ma Jian’s translation “列宿” (diagram C-A32-02); the stations-vs-crescent diagram (C-A32-03; the crescent idea is kept in E11); the scorpion’s crown/diadem; the four lucky stars and this weekend’s dawn Moon moving from “of the tents” to “the bucket’s front spout”; the Pegasus square = Shi and Bi = the bucket’s two spouts; the same-stars diagram C-A32-05; Niu (the ox) vs the Butcher and his sheep; tonight at 8 pm (Jupiter and Mars in the Lion’s eye and forehead, Spica “the raised one”, card C-A32-06); Taipei times and altitudes for the crescent; the closing two-ring disc and card C-A32-07; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “about 13° east”, “In 27 or 28 nights” = 03 鏡「每晚往東挪大約十三度……二十七八個晚上，就繞天一圈」.
2. E03 “a manzil: a lodging” = 「manzil——下榻處」.
3. E04/E05 = 06 鏡; “Around Scorpius / Then a flock of ostriches” keeps 「天蠍那一帶」「今天的人馬座那一帶」 (E05 drops the word Sagittarius; the label marks the place). “the Moon sleeps outdoors” = 「月亮那一晚，等於睡在野外」. “the empty place” follows the card; EN04 used “Open land” on a ring label — pick one in review.
4. E06 “The inn signs reached Europe” = 08 鏡「這些旅店的招牌，後來傳到歐洲」.
5. E08 “are now Libra” = 09 鏡「就是今天天秤座這兩顆」.
6. E09 “More than half of the 28 stations live on in official IAU star names” = 「二十八間裡，超過一半的名字，今天還寫在國際天文聯合會的正式星名表上」 (the card: 18 of 28).
7. E10 “Antares: in China, a star of Xin, ‘the Heart’” = 10b 鏡「天蠍的紅心，中國叫心宿二」 (心宿二 = the second star of the Heart mansion; “a star of Xin” avoids claiming it is the whole mansion).
8. E11 “traditionally” keeps 05 鏡「傳統上」; “Saudi Arabia’s calendar: New Year on Sunday 6 June” = 13 鏡「六月六號星期天，是沙烏地官方曆的伊斯蘭新年」 (not every country’s date).
9. E12 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of 13 鏡 (Taipei: 19:00–19:30, 14° up at 19:10, below 10° around 19:33). The Chinese computes Taipei only; “low in the west-northwest, just after sunset” is the general direction, and the Taipei numbers are removed from the labels. Reviewer: please confirm the wording does not promise a sighting.
10. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
11. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
