## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week" bridge; "it is January, inside the storytelling season" and the winter-calendar diagram; "if you see this in summer, save it" and the snakes-spiders-thunder reason (one telling); the Holy People and the sandpainting (kept only as the diagram's Mother Earth / Father Sky); Black God's line "this is called stars" as narration (kept in the diagram and headline); the North Fire, the Mongolian and Norse cross-references, the Revolving Male and Female and the house rule about two couples and one fire; the First Big One (Scorpius) and the planting signal; Dilyéhé as the ceremony clock; Black God's scolding of Coyote; the Window Rock 1.6° vs Taipei 12.3° diagram; the Taipei 11 p.m. sky tip and the "tonight" card (replaced by a Northern-Hemisphere tip); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E01 "stars scattered by Coyote" = 統一開頭「郊狼撒了一地的星星」.
2. E02 "sky stories are told only in winter — from first frost to first thunder" = 02 鏡. The Chinese script's 待查核 #1 is still open (a possible brief midsummer exception); if it is confirmed, both versions change to "traditionally" / "mainly".
3. E03 = 04 鏡「每個家族、每種儀式都有不同的版本；我們講的，是一本公開出版的書裡整理的那一個」 — the hedge that covers the whole story (待查核 #5: Childrey's retelling vs Haile 1947).
4. E05 "Black God stamped his foot" = 06 鏡「他跺一下腳……再跺」 (Childrey: four stamps; the diagram shows the five positions).
5. E07 "From a fawnskin pouch, he set them out" compresses 07–10 鏡 (the North Fire and the Revolving pair are cut, so only Orion is named); "In the west: the First Slim One — Orion" = 10 鏡「黑神又在西邊放了一個瘦長的人」.
6. E08 = 12 鏡「照著太陽穴上那團晶石，做了七顆小的放上天：Dilyéhé，昴宿」.
7. E09 "a fistful of crystal dust" = 13 鏡「一大把細碎的晶石，一揮手，撒過整片天」.
8. E10 / E11 = 14 鏡「你們在做什麼？怎麼沒問我！」「把剩下的晶石，全吹上了天」 (only the second question is used).
9. E12 = 15 鏡「天上大部分的星，都沒有名字，亂成一片……郊狼笑著說：『可是，天空變漂亮了啊！』」 (待查核 #5: whether Haile has this line is unconfirmed; covered by E03).
10. E13 "Many think it is Canopus" = 16 鏡「很多人認為，就是老人星」 (Childrey: “may possibly have been Canopus”). Hedge kept.
11. E14 = 17 鏡「一邊是黑神排好的規矩，一邊是郊狼撒出來的混亂。納瓦荷人說，世界本來就是這兩樣的平衡」.
12. E15 is a Northern-Hemisphere sky tip, not a translation: "under 2° high" in Navajo country = 16 鏡「在納瓦荷的家鄉，它最高離地平線不到兩度」; "below bright Sirius" = 19 鏡「天狼星下面低低的那顆」. North of about 37° N Canopus never rises; the line says "hides" and gives the Navajo-country figure rather than promising viewers a sighting.
13. Cover second line = the schedule's hook: "Coyote scattered the stars — and this story is told only in winter."
14. Page 61's frame is the Chinese Taipei view (Sirius and the Coyote Star in the south); E15 does not mention Taipei.
15. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 88 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
