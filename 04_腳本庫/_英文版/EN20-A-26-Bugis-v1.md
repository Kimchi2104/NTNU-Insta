## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last week in Brazil” bridge and the Tupi rhea; UNESCO 2017 on screen (kept in the YouTube description and caption sources); Ammarell's year on Balobaloang from 1991; the Widow's distances (4.4 vs 300+ light-years, shot 06); the goat in the narration (kept as a label); the Mandar Widow and the Sign of the Bajau (“neighbours in the sky”, shots 09–09b); the stingray and the shark and the three-week “same S” card (shot 11); the plough, the Sign of Three and the chicken manu' = Anuta's Manu (shot 12); the Austronesian spread from Taiwan (shot 13); the Taiwan viewing details (Taipei 19:30 altitudes, Canopus, Moon–Jupiter–Mars on 3/19, Crux and the Widow from Kenting); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “famous traders and sailors” = 「出了名的商人和水手」.
2. E04 “which star rises or sets over which island” compresses 「哪顆星會在哪座島、哪個港口的上方升起、停住或落下」 (stands and ports dropped for speed; the English diagram shows rises / stands / sets and the ports).
3. E05 “He called it a star compass” = 「Ammarell 說，這是一座星羅盤」 (his word, not the crews').
4. E06 “Their most-used guides” = 「他們最常用的」; “the Widow” is our short gloss of 「還沒出嫁就守寡的女子」 (Ôhashi: widow-before-marriage); the headline on page 19 carries the 「未婚夫在婚禮前死了」 part.
5. E08 “Late July, at dusk, facing south … From Balobaloang, she marks the way to Bima” = 「七月底、八月初的黃昏，從 Balobaloang 往南看，她就站在 Bima 港的方向」 (184.5°, between the two stars). Balobaloang is named only on the compass diagram; “early August” dropped for length. “Sailors set course by her” = 「水手靠她，定下出發的航向」.
6. E09 “one post is shorter than the other, so the house leans” = 「一根柱子比另一根短，整間歪一邊」.
7. E10 “At 5° S” = 「在南緯五度」 (the Chinese shows it from Makassar); “always below the horizon” = 「永遠在地平線下」.
8. E11 “North is marked by …” = 「指北的是北斗」; “like their own wooden sailboats” = 「就像他們自己的木造帆船」.
9. E12 “Seen from Sulawesi, it rises almost due east” = 「在蘇拉威西，它差不多從正東方升起」 (81°, 9° north of east — on the label).
10. E13 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of the Chinese 14 鏡 (「這週末晚上七點半，台灣往東北看，北斗這艘船斗柄朝下」); the Dipper's orientation depends on the hour, not the latitude, so “handle down” holds at mid-northern latitudes in the evening of late March. The page still shows Taipei's view.
11. E14 “The Mandar people see a fishing net around the Dipper” = 「北斗那一帶在他們眼裡，是一張漁網」 (the boat and three fishermen are left for EN21).
12. The schedule's hook cell for EN20 was still “to be set after Chinese v1”; this v0 sets it to *A compass with no needle — just stars.* (cover second line, card 07; also the caption's first line).
13. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 83 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive: blocks overlap across pages.
