## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last two episodes" bridge (dragon, palace); the Niu / Nü / Xu / Wei descriptions (all flagged ⚠ by the fact-check: Niu is not the Cowherd star, Nü is 57° from Vega, Wei is "rooftop" not "danger", "肅殺" is autumn not winter); the whole Luoshu section (Yu’s turtle, the Xia "Lou people", the Xia–Xuanwu link — fact-check ✗/⚠); the Southern Dipper vs Big Dipper comparison; the Korea teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode, EN45). The aired Canva has much more (the Arabic ostriches and lucky stars, the four Si- officials, the funeral asterisms, Shi as a building star and the *Book of Songs* line, Bi as the sky’s library, other peoples’ names for the Square, Tengshe running to the pole, the sky as a turning calendar) — none of it is in the repo’s Chinese v1 script, so none is used here.

Wording to check:
1. E01 "The Chinese saw" replaces the Chinese 「夏朝人看見」 (fact-check ⚠: no evidence of a Xia-dynasty Xuanwu; the image takes shape from the Warring States to the Han). "Not one animal, but two" = 05–15 s 「不是一種動物，是兩種」.
2. E02 "Guardian of the north — and of winter, water and death" = 「牠掌管北方，也掌管冬天、水，和死亡」.
3. E03–E04 "Look south in September … from Dou in the south to Shi and Bi in the east" = the aired Canva pages 3–4 and the script’s 「九月傍晚玄武七宿正當空；秋季四邊形入夜後升起」. The fact-check notes "north" is only the symbol’s name: at 9 pm the arc runs from Dou low in the south to Shi / Bi in the east — true for Northern mid-latitudes as well.
4. E05 "one still, one moving" = 「龜與蛇，一靜一動，合成玄武」; "North of the Room and the Wall" = 「在室宿和壁宿的北邊」.
5. E06 = 「室宿和壁宿這四顆星，西方人也連了起來——就是秋季四邊形，飛馬座的身體」; "in the West" kept as the Chinese says it.
6. E07 "45 stars" is from the Chinese first comment (羽林軍 45 星; fact-check ✓). "South of the Square" = 「而在它們南方」.
7. E08 (v0 “the lone bright star low in the south”, now “stands out low in the autumn south”): the Chinese says 「秋天的星空只有一顆亮星」; the fact-check notes this is a mid-latitude statement (from Taiwan, Achernar also shows low in the south, and Saturn is in the area). For the English edition’s Northern mid-latitude viewers it holds for stars; planets are not mentioned. "gate of the northern camp" = 「北方軍營的大門」.
8. E09 = 「龍在東、龜蛇在北。四象已經走了一半——接下來還有西方的白虎，和南方的朱雀」, told as one line (the English edition has already aired the tiger and the bird, EN03 and EN13).
9. The hook (cover’s second line) is new: the schedule said "to be set after the corrections"; it is the script’s own 「不是一種動物，是兩種」.
