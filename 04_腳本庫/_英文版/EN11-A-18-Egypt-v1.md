In the pyramid age that was a few days after the summer solstice; today, in Cairo, Sirius comes back around August 5, over a month later. ## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last week on the Great Plains” bridge; the 365-day civil calendar and its three seasons (05), the 1,460-year cycle and Censorinus’s year 139 (06, kept only in the first comment); Sah becoming Osiris and Sopdet Isis (09); the 36 decans and the 12 hours of the night (10); the Bull’s Foreleg (Big Dipper), the mooring post and the hippopotamus (11); the Imperishable Stars and the pyramid’s north entrance (12); the Great Pyramid squared to north (13); the cord-stretching rite (14); the Dendera zodiac, Napoleon’s scholars, Champollion and the Cleopatra-era date (15–17); the full Moon, Canopus and the Foreleg in the sky tip (18); the Taipei card C-A18-10; the Chinese next-Friday teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode, EN12 Arabia).

Wording to check:
1. E01 “a star that woke the Nile” = Chinese 統一開頭「一顆叫醒尼羅河的星」.
2. E02 “Egypt gets almost no rain. Life depends on the Nile.” = 02「埃及幾乎不下雨，活命全靠尼羅河」. The summer rains upstream and “from June” are only implied by the diagram (“Summer: the river rises”).
3. E04 present tense “sinks … each spring” = 03「每年春天，它沉進夕陽的光裡」 (a sky fact, not a past custom).
4. E06 “originally” = 04「原本就從這一天開始」, kept because the civil calendar drifted (Chinese note ⑤); “Around then, the river started rising” = 「差不多就在這時候，河水開始漲」.
5. E07 “a few days after the summer solstice, in time for the flood” = 07「大約在夏至後幾天，正好趕上漲水」; the diagram gives Jun 28–29 vs solstice Jun 24 (Gregorian, proleptic). “We did the math” = 「我們算了一下」.
6. E09 “doesn’t return until around August 5” = 07「要等到八月五日前後」 (US date order, as the diagram); E08 “over a month late” = 「晚了一個多月」 (38 days, our own computation, as in the Chinese).
7. E11 “In 1970 the Aswan High Dam was finished” = 08「一九七〇年亞斯文高壩完工」 (inaugurated Jan 1971 — the Chinese 考據備忘 note); “holds the yearly flood in a reservoir” = 「每年的大水就被攔進水庫」. The headline “The river no longer waits” = 題眼「等天狼星的河，已經不等了」.
8. E12 sky tip rewritten for northern mid-latitudes, late January: the Chinese says “10:30 tonight, Taipei, Sirius at its highest, 48°, Orion to its upper right”. The English drops the altitude (it depends on latitude) and says “North of the equator … around 22:00” (24-hour time; Sirius transits about 22:20 local mean time on Jan 27, and south of the equator the picture is mirrored).
9. E13 points to EN49 Tonga (Sun Jan 31), the next episode **by publish date** (card 07 rule, as EN09 → EN41); the earlier draft pointed to EN12 Ancient Arabia (Wed Feb 3). The end card therefore says “This Sunday”, and the caption’s last line follows.
10. The cover carries one line only (E01); the schedule’s hook is the caption’s first line.
11. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 90 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive where a block spans pages.

## 審稿合併 (Gemini review → v1)

Review table has 10 rows; each was checked against the Chinese v2 (A-18 逐字稿 v2). 8 adopted, 1 partly adopted, 2 not adopted (E01 counted as two points below). No new facts were taken.

Adopted:
1. E12 (🔴): “North of the equator, face south around 22:00: Sirius is highest, with Orion to its upper right.” 24-hour time, and the south-sky picture holds only in the north (Sirius is at dec −16.7°, so it transits south of the zenith north of the equator). The Chinese altitude (48°) stays out; it is for Taipei.
2. E07: “summer solstice” (Chinese 夏至; a bare “solstice” can read as winter). 
3. E09: “doesn’t return until around August 5” (US date order as the diagram; 要等到八月五日前後). YouTube description and caption changed to “August 5”.
4. E11: “Finished in 1970, the Aswan High Dam now holds the yearly flood in a reservoir.” “Finished” kept (the dam was inaugurated Jan 1971). YouTube: “is held in a reservoir”.
5. E06: “Around then, the river started rising. Egypt’s year originally began that day.” (no repeated “began”; w/s 2.0).
6. E04: two short sentences, the second one matching 03 鏡「每年春天，它沉進夕陽的光裡」.
7. Caption: “returned over Memphis” instead of “returned to Memphis”.
8. Caption first comment: “139 CE” (西元一三九年).
