## YouTube

- **Title**: Blackfoot: the Lost Children in the sky
- **Description**:
  On the Great Plains, some Blackfoot tipis carried a painted night sky: the Big Dipper and the Pleiades on a black band at the top. The Pleiades are the Lost Children: six poor children with no calf robes, mocked by the others, who got lost on the prairie and were taken into the sky. That is why they are missing in spring, while the calves are yellow, and come back in autumn.
  World Sky Cultures: the same sky, seen by different peoples. From the NTNU Astronomy Club, Taiwan. New episode every Wednesday.
  Sources: Walter McClintock, The Old North Trail (1910), pp. 217–220, 490; Wissler & Duvall, Mythology of the Blackfoot Indians (1908), for other versions of the story; Stellarium sky culture “blackfoot” (after D. S. Miller 1997).

## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last week’s Navajo episode; the dusty stars on screen (kept only on the diagram); the seven brothers and their sister (Big Dipper story, 05–07); the Dipper as a night clock, “the last brother” (08); the Pole Star as a hole in the sky (09); Feather Woman and the turnip (10); the Spider Man’s lodge (11); Scarface Poïa = Jupiter (12) and the two morning stars of 1905 (13); the dogs version of the Lost Children (16); the Wolf Trail line (17, kept as a label only); the Taipei tonight-and-tomorrow card with Venus and Jupiter (18); the Egypt bridge (19, replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check (as merged in v1):
1. E02 “Today: Alberta and Montana” = 02 鏡「今天分住在加拿大的亞伯達和美國的蒙大拿」.
2. E03 “Over a century ago, a writer counted one Great Plains summer camp: one in ten tipis was painted” = 02 鏡「以前」＋03 鏡「一百多年前……十頂帳篷就有一頂畫了圖」 (McClintock: 35 of 350 at one Sun Dance camp; written as one camp to answer the Chinese 待查核 #7 about over-generalising).
3. E05 “The designs came in dreams and belong to the owner. No one else may copy them.” = 04 鏡「這些圖案屬於主人，是夢裡得來的，別人不能照著畫」.
4. E06 “buffalo calves are yellow … After the hunt” = 14 鏡「春天獵到野牛，小牛皮是黃的」.
5. E07 “mangy old bulls” = 「長癬的老公牛」 (McClintock).
6. E08 “got lost on the prairie and were taken into the sky” = 15 鏡「在草原上走失，被帶上了天」 (passive kept; the Chinese does not say by whom).
7. E09/E10 “Gone while calves are yellow … In autumn, when the calves turn brown, the children return every night” = 15 鏡「春天，小牛還是黃的時候，天上看不到他們；等到秋天小牛轉成褐色，他們才每晚回來」. The diagram’s dates (May 1, Jun 24, Oct 10) are the Chinese diagram’s Browning computation.
8. E11 is rewritten for northern latitudes (24-hour time): the Chinese says the Pleiades are overhead at 20:00 in Taipei on 15 January. On 20 January they cross the meridian around 19:50 local time, about 66–76° up between 50° N and 40° N, so “ride high in the south” holds.
9. The schedule hook (“on top of the tipi”, as the Chinese note corrected from “at the door”) is not on the cover any more; it opens the caption.
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 80 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
