## YouTube

- **Title**: Matariki: when the stars rise, the new year begins
- **Description**:
  Each April and May the Pleiades — Matariki — vanish into the Sun’s glare. In June they return at dawn, in the southern winter, and for the Māori a new year begins. Since 2022 New Zealand has made it a public holiday, its date set by the stars and the Moon: this year, Friday 25 June. Many accounts count seven stars; in recent years nine get the most attention, each watching over food, water, weather, health, wishes — or the dead.
  World Sky Cultures: the same sky, seen by different peoples. From the NTNU Astronomy Club, Taiwan. New episode every Wednesday.
  Sources: Rangi Matamua, Matariki: The Star of the Year (2017); NZ Government (employment.govt.nz); Matariki Advisory Group dates 2022–2052; Te Papa; Te Ara; Stellarium sky culture (maori, Dan Smale).

## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday (asterisms); the solstice daylight comparison (Wellington 9.2 h vs Taipei 13.7 h, diagram C-A33-03); “Taiwan sees it four or five days before New Zealand” (diagram C-A33-04); reading the year from how clear the cluster looks; the nine-star card C-A33-05; the hautapu ceremony and the comparison with Chinese New Year (C-A33-06); Puanga / Rigel on the west coast and Puaka (C-A33-08); tonight’s Antares (Rehua) and the Taipei times for tomorrow’s dawn (card C-A33-07); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (the Milky Way, X-07; replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode, EN34 asterisms — in English the order of A-36 and A-33 is swapped).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “Since 2022 … has had a public holiday called Matariki” = 02 鏡「二〇二二年起，紐西蘭多了一個國定假日，叫 Matariki」. The headline “This Friday” is true for the English publish date (Wed 23 June → Fri 25 June); the Chinese airs on the day.
2. Diagram C-A33-01 (English) keeps the government wording verbatim: “the first public holiday to recognise Te Ao Māori”, with “(the Māori world view)” in place of the Chinese gloss.
3. E04 “Each April and May, it sinks into the evening twilight and hides in the Sun’s glare” = 03 鏡「每年四、五月，它在黃昏沉進西方，躲進太陽的光裡」.
4. E05 “in the southern winter. The old year ends; a new one begins” = 03 鏡「六月，再從冬天的黎明裡冒出來」＋05 鏡「舊的一年結束，新的一年開始」.
5. E06 + diagram C-A33-02: the caption now reads “Official rule: the Friday closest to Tangaroa in Pipiri” — “Official” keeps 04 鏡「官方採用的毛利月曆」 (each iwi’s maramataka differs). The Chinese 「大約下弦月前後」 is shown only as the last-quarter symbol on 27 June.
6. E08 “Many accounts count seven” = 08 鏡「很多說法只數七顆」; E09 “Lately, nine get the most attention” = 「近年最受注目的，是九顆星的說法」 (not “there are really nine”). “drew them from a 1933 family manuscript” = 「整理祖先在一九三三年完成的手稿，介紹這九顆星」; the Chinese caption calls it 家族手稿.
7. E10 domains shortened: 「土裡長的食物」 → food from the soil; 「樹上的果實和鳥」 → fruit and birds; 「淡水和河裡的食物」 → fresh water; 「大海和海裡的食物」 → the sea.
8. E11 “Matariki, the mother: health” = 10 鏡「Matariki 是母親，照看人們的健康」; “new year wishes” = 「收下新年的願望」; “Pōhutukawa: those lost this past year” = 「代表過去一年離開的人」.
9. E12 “you need a truly dark sky” = 「要在很黑的地方才看得到」.
10. E13 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of 13 鏡 (Taipei, 26 June 03:30–04:00, east-northeast, 10°→17°). Checked with PyEphem for 24 June when the Sun is 12° below the horizon: Alcyone 17° up at 25° N, 14° at 35° N, 11° at 40° N, 8° at 45° N, azimuth 64–71° — so “low in the east-northeast” holds across the mid-latitudes. “as New Zealand greets its new year” = 「跟紐西蘭一起迎新年」.
11. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 90 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
