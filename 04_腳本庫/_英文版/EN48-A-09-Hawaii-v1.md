## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week" bridge and the 50th-anniversary line; Nainoa's 1980 voyage and "first Hawaiian navigator in 600-odd years"; the star compass (32 houses, Mintaka rising due east); the spine's middle stars (Meʻe, Hikianalia, Nā Hiku); the meridian pointers; the fishing line, Māui's fishhook and the Māori names; the navigator's triangle (Deneb, Vega, Altair) and the Polynesian Triangle; Kawelo's kite and its four chiefs' stars; the Siberian cross-reference; Makahiki's truce and games; "star lines are not a GPS" (dead reckoning); the Taipei 8 p.m. sky tip (replaced by a Northern-Hemisphere one); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E01 "four star lines home" = 「四條回家的路」; present tense "see" (the Chinese has no tense, and past tense read as “the ancients saw”); the hedge comes in E07 (「這不是古代傳下來的星圖，是現代航海家……做的筆記」).
2. E02 "no compass, no sextant, no map" = 02 鏡「沒有羅盤、沒有六分儀、沒有地圖」. The Chinese 「全靠看天、看海」 (stars plus swells, wind, clouds, birds) is dropped rather than shortened to "by the stars alone", which would overclaim.
3. E03 "is Arcturus — ‘star of gladness’" = 02 鏡「船名 Hōkūleʻa，就是這顆大角星——『喜悅之星』」.
4. E04 / map: "over 4,000 km (4,355 km direct)" = 「四千多公里」＋首留言 great-circle 4,355 km; "34 days" = 「三十四天後」.
5. E05 "By then, Hawaiʻi’s deep-sea wayfinding was long lost" = 03 鏡「那時夏威夷人的遠洋導航，早已失傳」; "navigator" = 「航海師」.
6. E06 "His student Nainoa Thompson split the sky into four north–south star lines" = 05 鏡; the Chinese says Nainoa learned from Mau (03 鏡「他的學生 Nainoa Thompson」).
7. E07 "Not an ancient chart … notes for memorising the sky" = 「現代航海家為了背天空，做的筆記」 (hedge kept).
8. E08 "From the Southern Cross, past Hōkūleʻa, up to Hōkūpaʻa" = 06 鏡 (Meʻe, Hikianalia and Nā Hiku skipped).
9. E09 seasons = 07 鏡 exactly (winter bailer, spring backbone, summer fishing line, autumn kite). "chief’s" follows the Chinese 「酋長的釣線」 (lani = sky, also chief).
10. E11 / E12 = 09 鏡: 「船往北開，看它一晚比一晚高，等它正好掛在頭頂，就到了夏威夷的緯度」; the Southern Cross ruler appears only in the diagram (≈ 6°, near 21° N).
11. E13 "From Capella to Sirius, it scoops up Orion and the Pleiades as it rises" = 13 鏡「從北端的五車二……彎到最亮的天狼星……勺子裡盛著獵戶座和昴宿星團……從東邊升起時盛滿星星」. "Winter’s line" = 07 鏡「冬天是 Makaliʻi 的舀水杓」.
12. E14 = 14 鏡「每年十一月，Makaliʻi 在日落時從東方升起，Makahiki 季就開始了——四個月獻給豐收之神 Lono」. Headline "the new-year season": the Chinese 「新年鐘」 is the Pleiades (Makaliʻi), not Makahiki, so the headline names the season.
13. E15 is a new Northern-Hemisphere sky tip (rule for the English edition), not a translation: around 20:00 on 10 January at 40° N the Pleiades are near the meridian, roughly 70°+ up (hand-computed: LST ≈ 3.4 h vs RA 3.8 h). The Chinese Taipei tip (11/6 20:00) does not apply to a January post.
14. Hook (new, the schedule had none): "1976: no compass, no map — 34 days to Tahiti." Written into the schedule's hook column; used in the caption, not on the cover.
15. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
