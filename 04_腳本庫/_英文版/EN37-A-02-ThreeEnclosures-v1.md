## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

**Three known errors in the Chinese A-02 (fact-check report 2026-10-28) — the English avoids all three:**
1. Chinese 46–66 s 「最中間那顆叫『帝』」 ✗ (Di is the **second** of the five; the middle one is Shuzi). E05 says only “One of them is called Di” and the page 10 caption is deleted.
2. Chinese 66–82 s 「連……上廁所的地方都有星官」 ✗ (no toilet asterism in Ziwei). Not used.
3. Chinese 124–136 s 「它的西牆末端，就是……五帝座一」 ✗ (Denebola is inside Taiwei, the first star of the Thrones of the Five Emperors). E11 says “The Thrones’ main star is Denebola”.

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last time we built a dragon” bridge; Gouchen, Tianhuang Dadi, the canopy (Huagai), the officials, the bed and the other court asterisms; the Three Dukes and officials of Taiwei; the market tower, shops and measures (only “a market” and “the Emperor goes shopping” stay); the 31 sky regions (the 28 mansions stay); the next-Friday Black Tortoise teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode). Also dropped: everything that is only on the aired Canva pages and not in the script (the seven cultures “this star does not move”, the Han-dynasty wording, Thuban 4,700 years ago, the Dipper as the Emperor’s chariot, the Dipper and Corona Borealis in other cultures, Denebola’s Arabic and Romanian names, Lingtai, the kitchens, the Suzhou planisphere numbers, which the report flags ⚠).

Wording to check:
1. E03 “Beijing’s Forbidden City is named after it” = 「人間的紫禁城，名字正是從這裡來的」; the report: the usual account (通行說法). Hedged in v1 (“is said to be named after it”; 16 w / 7 s = 2.3).
2. E04 “like arms” = 「像兩隻手臂圍成一座城」.
3. E07 “3,000 years ago, the Sky Emperor lived in Di: the star Kochab” = 「三千年前的天帝住在北極二」; report: in 1000 BCE β UMi was 6.5° from the pole and the brightest star near it — checked. The aired Canva says 漢代; the English follows the script.
4. E08 “a full circle every 26,000 years” = 「兩萬六千年繞一圈」 (report: ~25,800, acceptable). “has moved” = 「天帝，其實搬過家」.
5. E09 “in the spring sky” = 「在春天的南方天空」 (“southern” dropped; E14 places it).
6. E10 “At its heart sit the Thrones of the Five Emperors” = 「裡面有五帝座」 + the aired page 29 「正中央是五帝座」.
7. E11 “a lion’s tail and an emperor’s seat” = 「同一顆星，中國人拿它當皇帝的座位」; “first star” = 五帝座一.
8. E12 “Even the Sky Emperor goes shopping” = 「天帝也要逛市場」.
9. E13 “a human world, projected onto the sky” = 「一座投影在天上的人間」.
10. E14 is written for about 40° N, early May, 21:30 local time (PyEphem, 2027-05-09): Denebola 62° up in the south-south-west (Taiwei “high in the south”), Kochab 53° in the north (Ziwei), Rasalgethi and Rasalhague 20–25° in the east (Tianshi “rising”).
