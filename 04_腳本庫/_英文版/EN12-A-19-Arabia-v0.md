## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last week’s Egypt episode and the December A-13 star calendar (with the C-A19-01 two-layers diagram); six of the 14 animals in the list (sheep, goats, caracal, sandgrouse, larks, doves); the whole Suhail / Canopus half — the Yemen star and the 37° N limit (C-A19-03), the two sisters Sirius and Procyon (al-ʿAbūr, al-Ghumayṣāʾ), the Milky Way check and the 5.4× brightness, the toll-collector story and its unreliable chain (C-A19-04), 310 light-years and the Chinese Old Man of the South Pole (C-A19-05); the this-weekend Taipei viewing (21:43, 12°, Kenting 15°; C-A19-06) and the glossary card (C-A19-07); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by Saturday’s English special).

No sky-watching tip: the Chinese tip is for Canopus, which is not in this story and never rises north of about 37° N; the ostrich stars (Fomalhaut, Diphda) have set by mid-evening in early February. Nothing was invented to replace it.

Wording to check:
1. E01 “the ancient Arabs” = 「古阿拉伯人」.
2. E02 “Around the rise of Islam … Later scholars wrote them down” = 「伊斯蘭興起前後，貝都因人的星名藏在古詩裡，八到十世紀的學者，一句一句抄下來」 (dates dropped for reading speed).
3. E03 “100-plus constellations, many are animals … over 20 kinds” = 「這一百多組星座，很多是動物……加起來二十多種」; list shortened to eight.
4. E05 “Legend says” keeps 「傳說」.
5. E06 “Alula, Tania, Talitha — first, second, third leap” = 「今天的星名……意思就是第一跳、第二跳、第三跳」.
6. E07 “Diphda” = 土司空 (β Cet) under its IAU name; “the chicks” = 「一群小鴕鳥」.
7. E08 “a few mother ostriches” = 「鯨魚座還有幾隻鴕鳥媽媽」 (new-version English: *The mother ostriches*).
8. E10 compresses 「今天的正式名字叫 Azha——就是阿拉伯文的『鴕鳥窩』，中世紀抄書的人抄錯了一個字母」; “medieval” is on the diagram.
9. E13 “walk down to the Milky Way to drink” = 「四隻走下銀河這條大河喝水」.
10. E14 “died out in the 20th century” = 「二十世紀就滅絕了」 (no year given on purpose: 1941 vs 1966 is disputed).; 「天上的，還在」 → “In the sky, they remain.”
11. Page 3 → page 7 is a Match & Move between two different framings of the same winter sky (the Chinese goes 3 → 4–6 same frame → 7), so the move is the same one the Chinese makes from page 6.
