## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the Marquis Yi of Zeng lacquer box (c. 433 BCE: the 斗 character, the earliest complete list of the 28 lunar mansions, dragon and tiger on the lid — shots 06, pages 17–21); the two readings of the box’s north side (black panel = Black Tortoise / two beasts facing three stars = qilin — shot 07, pages 22–24); the club’s view that the four directions reflect four peoples, with the qilin possibly from the Donghu (shot 08, pages 25–27); the “360 of each kind” detail (kept only in the diagram footer); the names of the four animal flags’ star groups (kept only in the diagram); the 9 stars of later Hushi star lists (diagram only); the one-page summary card (page 44, Chinese only); the 參考文獻 page (page 47 — Chinese sources; replaced by the YouTube description and first comment); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode, EN08 Orion).

Wording to check:
1. Cover second line = the schedule hook, set in this pass (the schedule had 「依中文 v2 再定」): *Four beasts guard China’s sky. Who sits in the middle?* E01 / cover: the Chinese opening is 「同一片星空，古人數過五隻神獸——其中一隻，不見了。」 English keeps the series *One sky. …* opening but says “counted” (數過) and drops the em-dash split; page 1 is held 4 s (Chinese 5 s) so it reads at 2.5 w/s.
2. E02 “one sacred beast for each direction” compresses 「東青龍、南朱雀、西白虎、北玄武——四象」; the diagram names all four.
3. E05 drops the articles (“Feathered: phoenix …”) to stay at 2.0 w/s; the diagram rows show the same names as they appear.
4. E06 “The fifth: no feathers, no scales” = 「倮之蟲——沒有羽毛，也沒有鱗甲」; “the sage — a human” = 「聖人，也就是人」.
5. E07 “No tiger — the qilin instead” = 「西方的白虎不在名單裡，毛蟲之長是麒麟」. The diagram footer keeps the Chinese caveat (two different systems; the pairing is illustrative).
6. E08 quotes the Yueling loosely: 「中央土……其帝黃帝……其蟲倮」 → “gives the centre to the Yellow Emperor and ‘naked’ creatures. That means humans.” (the last sentence = 「中央，是人的位置」). The diagram gives the fuller quote.
7. E09 repeats the diagram’s question (“Which stars belong to the centre?”) in shorter words; page 30 is held 4.5 s (Chinese 3 s) so it reads at 2.4 w/s.
8. E09 / E15 / captions keep the Chinese hedges: the candidates are 「社團列了三個候選」 (our club’s), the Xuanyuan link is a 「線索」 (clue), and 「沒有任何傳世文獻明說」 = “No surviving text says”.
9. E11 “around Leo” = 「在獅子座一帶」; “exactly 17 stars” = 「星數正好十七顆」. “Count them.” is a stage invitation (production note: let viewers count), not a claim.
10. E12 “a rare sky story about people” = 「天上少數有『人』的故事」 (the diagram subtitle says “one of the few sky stories about people”).
11. E13 “Five chariot flags matched stars: four animals, one human-made bow” compresses the Kaogongji passage; the diagram shows the five flags and their star groups.
12. E14 “four stars called the Bow, ‘facing the Wolf’” = 《史記》「下有四星曰弧，直狼」; “It aims at Sirius” = 「這把弓，對著天狼星」.

Caption sky tip is new wording for the Northern Hemisphere (series rule), not a new claim about the stars: computed for 40° N on 2027-01-03 (Vega 21° / Altair 17° in the west at 18:00; Sirius rises ESE ≈ 19:00; Regulus rises E ≈ 21:00).
