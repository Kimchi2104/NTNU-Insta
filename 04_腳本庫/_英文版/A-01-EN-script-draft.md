1. **Opening Hook**: Adapted the conversational Chinese opening into the mandatory series formula *"One sky. The ancient Chinese saw — an Azure Dragon."*
2. **Pacing and Fat Trimming**: Reduced explanatory remarks about fist measurement and clan totems (Taihao clan lore from Zuo Zhuan condensed) to keep the narrative brisk and under the 180-second limit.
3. **Asterism Nomenclature**: Ensured all Chinese star groupings are strictly identified as *asterisms* rather than *constellations*, providing Pinyin alongside poetic translations on first mention (*Jiao, "the Horns"*; *Xin, "the Heart"*).
4. **Scorpius Comparison**: Retained the core cross-cultural insight comparing Dahuo with Antares (Rival of Mars), showing how distinct civilizations projected a burning heart onto the exact same red supergiant.
