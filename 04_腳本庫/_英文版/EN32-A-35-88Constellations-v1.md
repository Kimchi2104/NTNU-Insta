  The 88 constellations started with Ptolemy’s 48. ## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday (al-Ṣūfī); “over 1,000 stars” and the Almagest by name; the Alexandria mask (shot 03: “dark stars left between the figures”; kept only as “never saw the far southern sky”); Keyser, de Houtman and Bayer 1603; Danzig and “seven survive today” (kept in the headline “Hevelius: 7 more”); the shield for the Polish king (Scutum); the dropped constellations (the oak, the wall quadrant, the cat; “same star, different map, different constellation”); “the three-year-old IAU” and Rome; “Ori is Orion”; the Argentine star atlas used for the southern borders; Serpens split in two (89 pieces); the 1875 coordinates and today’s slight tilt; the four constellations never seen from Taipei and the corner of Apus from Eluanbi; China’s 283 asterisms and “88 is one way among many”; tonight’s Serpens and Scutum from Taipei; all five diagrams/cards (C-A35-01 to -05); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “Today’s 88 began with Ptolemy’s 48, listed nearly 1,900 years ago” = 02 鏡「這套星座的老底子，是將近一千九百年前……托勒密……列的四十八個星座」.
2. E03 “never saw the far southern sky” = 03 鏡「托勒密看不到南天最南的那一塊」 (from Alexandria, the place is in the cut mask only).
3. E04 “In the late 1500s, Dutch navigators charted the south” = 04 鏡「十六世紀末，荷蘭船隊開到南半球，領航員……記下南天的星」; E05 “Plancius made 12 new constellations” = 「Plancius 把它們做成十二個新星座」.
4. E06 = 05 鏡; “filled northern and equatorial gaps” = 「北邊和赤道一帶的空地」; “Hevelius’s 1690 atlas” (he died in 1687; the atlas was published in 1690); headline “7 more” = 「有七個留到今天」.
5. E07 “measured nearly 10,000 stars at the Cape of Good Hope” (“southern” dropped: the Cape already says it) = 06 鏡「他在南非好望角量了將近一萬顆南天星」; E08 “almost all instruments” = 「幾乎全是儀器」.
6. E09 “Table Mountain — Mensa” = 06b 鏡「開普敦的桌山，就是山案座」.
7. E10 “the new International Astronomical Union” = 「剛成立三年的」; “set the list: 88, each with a three-letter code” = 「定下八十八個星座的名單，每個配三個字母的縮寫」. The list is 1922, the borders 1928/1930 (Chinese ⚠: the common “88 were fixed in 1928” is wrong).
8. E11–E12 “Delporte drew them: straight lines along the sky’s grid” = 09 鏡「邊界全部沿著赤經、赤緯畫成直線」; E13 “Approved in 1928, published in 1930” = 09c 鏡.
9. E14 = 10 鏡「天上每一點，都屬於、也只屬於一個星座」; E15 “19 times the size of” = 1,302.84 ÷ 68.45 = 19.0 (Chinese 自算).
10. E16 = EN33’s schedule title, *Matariki: When the Stars Rise, the New Year Begins*.
11. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), a translation of the Chinese schedule hook 「1930 年，一本書把天空切成 88 塊」 without “a book” (Delporte’s borders were published as a book, but the cover would invite “which book?”).
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 90 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
