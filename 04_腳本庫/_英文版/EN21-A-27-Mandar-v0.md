  On the west coast of Sulawesi, the Bugis see the Big Dipper as a ship — and call two of its stars “the Mandar Widow”. Almost every star pattern the Mandar recorded is at sea. But researchers only asked fishermen on the coast; what inland rice farmers see, no one has asked yet.

## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday’s Bugis episode; the trade routes and monsoon sailing seasons (diagram layers C-A27-01 routes/monsoon); the 2021 date and the bibliography card (C-A27-02; the date stays in the caption sources); the southern sky — the Slanted House, the two Widows (red and green), the stingray and the 17-star shark as monsoon signs (C-A27-03 calendar); the Pleiades and flying fish, the group of three, the Milky Way dragon; Venus’s two names (C-A27-04); the Bugis farming constellations and the two-maps card (C-A27-05; only “almost all at sea” and the inland-farmers caveat are kept); the Austronesian origin and the Karama valley (C-A27-06); the Taipei 26 Mar times, the same-moment diagram (C-A27-07) and the look-up card (C-A27-08); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 = 01 鏡「上週，布吉斯人在北斗上看見一艘船……曼達爾漁夫看見——一張漁網」 without “last week”; “next door” = 07 鏡/11 鏡「鄰居」.
2. E04 = 02 鏡「曼達爾人住在蘇拉威西西岸，面對望加錫海峽。他們是漁夫，也是商人」.
3. E05 = 03 鏡「印尼萬隆理工學院的天文學家：他們訪問了一群七十歲以上的退休漁夫……年輕一輩出海，已經不看星星了」; “all over 70” = 「七十歲以上」 (the paper: every informant over 70).
4. E06 = 07 鏡「布吉斯人……還把斗口這兩顆叫『曼達爾的寡婦』」; “these two bowl stars” = Dubhe and Merak (the red line on page 20).
5. E07 = 07 鏡「這兩顆也在一艘船上——只是船身不往斗柄走，而是一路拉進天龍座，接到牧夫座」.
6. E08 = 08 鏡「北斗剩下的五顆：中間三顆是漁夫，兩頭各一顆是漁具」.
7. E09 keeps the attribution 「老漁夫說」 = “Old fishermen say”; 「四月半夜看見它」 = “see it late at night in April” (the Chinese deliberately avoids “rises” and “high”, and so does the English); 「涼季就要來了」 = “the cool season is near”; 「一種叫 bambang 的魚也會出現」 = “A fish called bambang appears”.
8. E10 = 11 鏡「曼達爾記下來的星座，幾乎都在海上」.
9. E11 = 11 鏡「研究者也提醒：他們只問了海邊的漁夫——內陸種稻的曼達爾人看見什麼，還沒有人去問」.
10. E12–E13 replace the Taipei 26 Mar tip (13 鏡); they are not in the Chinese script. Checked for 31 Mar, 21:00 local time at 40° N: Dubhe 64° (az 25°), Merak 68°, κ Dra 52°, Thuban 45°, Alkaid 45°, θ Boo 40° — all eleven stars of the net are 40° or more up, high in the northeast. E12’s “one end of the Mandar net” = 13 鏡「北斗——曼達爾漁網的那一頭」. E13 = the Chinese note 「整艘船貼著北方低空」 (in Majene the net never gets higher than 17–37°); the Chinese 8 pm numbers (bowl ~20°, stern below the sea) are a Taiwan-time comparison and are not used.
11. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook (written for this v0: the Chinese hook 「同一個北斗：布吉斯人看見船，曼達爾人看見漁網」).
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
