## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last week’s Arabia episode; the Four Symbols on the north disc and the Book of Rites marching order (C-A20-06); Zhen = a carriage (label only); the three Quail parts by mansion (labels only) and the Erya line; the Zuozhuan fire officers and the two “fires” (C-A20-01); Shaohao naming offices after birds (the hedged inference); the New Year’s Eve feast in the sky (cook, kitchen, firewood, wine flag, gruel jar, Gui’s sacrifices); the Shang hymn as narration (kept on the diagram); Mars, Regulus and the −2.6 magnitude in the narration (kept as labels), both planets at opposition; the midnight Quail Fire crossing and the New Year’s Eve card (C-A20-05); the Beehive Cluster and the Gui-Metal-Goat; the Western Hydra, Crater and Corvus; the Four Symbols card (C-A20-04); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by the next English episode, 17 Feb).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “four great beasts … the last of them” = 「四象只差最後一隻」; “of the south” = 南方朱雀.
2. E03 “Sima Qian: Liu the beak, Xing the neck, Zhang the crop, Yi the wings” = 「柳是鳥嘴，星宿是脖子，張是嗉囊，翼是翅膀」 (v1 correction: Xing is the neck).
3. E04 “Its middle third: Chunhuo” = 「柳、星、張是鶉火」; the Chinese notes that by the Jin shu degrees Liu mostly falls in Quail Head — the common simplification is kept, as in the Chinese.
4. E06 = 「下這顆蛋的不是朱雀」.
5. E07/diagram: swallow is attributed to the commentaries (毛傳、鄭箋), not stated as fact.
6. E08 “the Great Fire, the Azure Dragon’s heart” = 「青龍的心：大火」 (商主大火).
7. E10 “The Guoyu says” keeps 「《國語》記著」; whether it is a first-hand record is debated (on the diagram).
8. E11 “Look east after dark: it’s the brightest light” = 「晚上八點往東看：最亮的那顆是木星」, made general for the Northern Hemisphere: Jupiter reaches opposition on 11 Feb, so it rises at dusk everywhere at mid-northern latitudes.
9. E12 next episode = EN14 (Wed 17 Feb), the next English release by date; there is no English episode on 10 Feb.
10. Diagrams: C-A20-02 quote shortened to “Heaven sent the dark bird down to bear Shang” (天命玄鳥，降而生商); C-A20-03 quotes shortened to “…a crow, red in colour” and “Jupiter was in Quail Fire” so they fit beside the drawings.
11. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 79 s); the Sec column in Beats is not additive.
