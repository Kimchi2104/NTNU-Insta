## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last week, three fishermen” bridge and the New Year framing; “hard to find another group this neat” (kept as “similarly bright stars in a straight line”); Egypt’s Sah and the Pyramid Texts (Sah kept as a name only); the Babylonian “true shepherd of Anu”, Greek Orion and the scorpion, Arabic al-Jawzā (shot 04); the whole “one leg” thread — Tupi, Lokono, Tikuna (shots 05–07, the second story of the episode); Mabukuli / the tapir’s jaw; the review of earlier episodes and the “names of the Belt” card (shot 11); the young blue giants: brightness, 1,200+ light-years, a few million years, the nebula still forming stars (shot 12); the Taipei “tonight” card and moonrise (shot 13, rewritten for mid-northern latitudes); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. Cover second line = E01 (*One sky. Three stars the whole world knows.*). The schedule hook *China: Shen. Egypt: Sah. The Kamilaroi: Birray Birray.* lives in the schedule and caption; on screen E02 says it as a sentence on pages 2–3.
2. E03 “under 3° across” = 「前後不到三度」 (2.74° measured); “similarly bright” = 「差不多亮」 (V 2.41, 1.69, 1.77).
3. E04 “Almost on the celestial equator” = 「差不多就躺在天赤道上」 (Mintaka dec −0.3°); “from the Arctic to New Zealand” = the Chinese 北極圈、台灣、紐西蘭 (“everywhere” dropped: the Chinese list is three places, and at the poles the equator stars skim the horizon) (Taipei stays in the diagram).
4. E05 “three young boys” = 「還沒成年的男孩」; the Chinese says 「下面那把劍，是撥火的棍子」 → “the Sword, their fire stick”.
5. E06 “Three stars” = 參宿一、參宿六、參宿七 (named on the diagram, not in the text). “creation’s fire” = 「創世的那一把火」; “triangle” = the Chinese 動畫備註「推近三角形」 (the three stones are 參宿一、六、七, not the line of the Belt).
6. E07 “wooden fire drill” = 「鑽火的木棍」. It drops 「曆法走完一輪」 (calendar round) to stay under 2.5 w/s; “priests drilled a new one on a hilltop” = 「祭司在山頂鑽出新的火」. The diagram caption keeps the calendar wording.
7. E08 = 「阿茲特克人說…鑽不出來，太陽就不會再升起」; names the Aztecs because it stays up over three diagram pages.
8. E10 is new wording for the Northern Hemisphere (series rule), not a new claim: computed for 40° N, 2027-01-06 20:00 — Belt 36° up at azimuth 130° (southeast), Betelgeuse 40° / 119°, Rigel 33° / 140°, Sirius 13° / 126°. The Chinese gives Taipei 1/1 20:00 east-southeast, with Sirius 「腰帶正下方」; at 40° N Sirius sits below and a little left of the Belt, so the text says “Brightest below”, not “directly below”. Re-checked with PyEphem (40° N, 2027-01-06 20:00 local): Betelgeuse 40°/119°, Rigel 33°/140°, Sirius 14°/126° — matches. The page-31 background is the Taipei view; same layout.
9. Pages 2–3: the hook stays as a sentence over the names; the labels under it already show 參／Sah／Birray Birray, so the top box must not cover them (labels are at Y 1351–1437).
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing. The Sec column in Beats is not additive across overlapping pages.
