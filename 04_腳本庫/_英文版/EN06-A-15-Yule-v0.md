## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week in the Arctic, these stars were dogs" bridge (the Inuit dogs kept only in E09); King Hákon's beer law in the text (kept on the diagram); Odin as Jólnir and the eight reindeer / Sleipnir myth-busting (diagram C-A15-02 not used); "few names survive", the Latin word list, the sky as a giant's skull and the stars as sparks; "two rows of stars like open jaws"; the Moon passing every month and the Sun in early summer (E08 keeps only "beside the road"); Lokono in the text (label only); the Fishermen and Frigg's distaff in the text (labels only); Þjazi's eyes and Aurvandil's toe; Trondheim's long night, the Man's and Woman's Wagons, the Guiding Star and Polaris 1,000 years ago; the Norse star-name dictionary card; the Taipei "this weekend" guide and the Christmas Moon by Gemini; the Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. Cover: "The Vikings saw — a wolf’s jaw" = 「同一片星空，維京人看見——一張狼嘴」. The cover's second line is the schedule's hook (*Before Christmas there was Yule — and a wolf's jaw among the stars.*), not a translation.
2. E02 "Scandinavians still call Christmas jul" = 「北歐人到今天還把聖誕節叫 jul」 (Swedish/Norwegian/Danish jul; Icelandic jól is on the diagram). "The old English word is Yule" = 「英文的古字是 Yule」.
3. E03 "reached the North before Christianity: three nights from midwinter" = 「這個節比基督教更早到北方：從仲冬那一夜開始，一連過三晚」.
4. E05 "A late-1100s Icelandic manuscript names this V of stars" compresses 「一本十二世紀末的冰島手抄本，在一份拉丁文詞彙表裡，替幾個星座註上北歐名字」+「畢宿的 V 字，叫 Úlfs kjaptr——狼嘴」.
5. E06 "the sky is always on the run" = 「北歐神話裡的天空，一直在逃命」.
6. E08 keeps the hedge 「所以有人猜，它等著咬的，就是日月」 = "So some guess it waits to bite them." The manuscript gives only the name.
7. E09 "Babylon: a bull’s jaw" = 「古巴比倫叫它『公牛的下顎』」; "the Tikuna: a crocodile’s mouth"; "the Tupi: a tapir’s jaw" (the Chinese adds the Lokono, kept on the label); "The Inuit saw dogs" = 「上週的因紐特人，看到的則是一群狗」 ("last week" dropped). The headline "Others saw a jaw too" covers the first three; the Inuit line is the contrast, as in the Chinese 「則是」.
8. E10 is the one rewritten line (Northern-Hemisphere mid-latitudes, not Taipei): the Chinese has Taipei 25 Dec 20:00, Aldebaran 57° up in the east and the Belt to its lower right. At 35–50° N on a late-December evening the Hyades are likewise climbing in the east with the Belt below them; "below it" simplifies 「右下方」. Please check this is acceptable.
9. Diagram C-A15-01 (English) "Everyone must have a share of beer, or pay a fine — the feast lasts as long as the beer" = 「每個人都得備好一份啤酒，不然就罰錢——酒喝多久，節就過多久」. Diagram C-A15-03: Árvakr “Early Riser”, Alsviðr “Very Swift”, Hrímfaxi’s foam "from its bit" (the diagram's 嚼子; the narration says 嘴邊).
