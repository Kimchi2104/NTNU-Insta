## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the C-01 recap (“last time, three schools argued about the shape of the sky”); the shadow reasoning (shots 06–07: earth north → Sun lower → longer shadow; this was our own geometric inference, not the text's words, so it is the first thing to go); Galileo's flies, butterflies, fish bowl and dripping bottle (only in the Chinese first comment anyway); Yao Xin of Wu and the body–sky analogy, including his explanation of winter and summer (shots 14–16); the closing summary card (shot 17); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Kept on purpose: the 13 鏡 line the Chinese marks 「本段不可剪（學術界定）」 — E10 + E11, and the diagram on pages 26–27 says it in full.

Wording to check:
1. E01 “the earth always moves — unnoticed” = 「大地一直在動，只是你不知道」. The schedule's hook (cover second line, card 07) is now *The earth never stops moving. Why can’t you feel it?* (= 「地恆動不止，而人不知」); as the schedule asks, “earlier than Galileo” is not used as a hook anywhere.
2. E02 “makes a bold claim” = 「給了一個大膽的答案」; “a Han-dynasty text” = 漢代的緯書 (the word “apocrypha” is kept for the description).
3. E03 “30,000 li up, north and west … Summer: back the other way” = 「冬至，大地往上、往北、往西，移動三萬里；夏至反過來，往下、往南、往東，也是三萬里」. The diagram shows the original wording line by line.
4. E04 “Once a year, there and back” = 「一年來回一趟」.
5. E05 “The text goes on” = 「書裡接著說」; the quote itself is on the diagram (“The earth never stops moving, yet people do not know it.”). The question “So why don’t we feel it?” comes from the dropped shot 07 (「那為什麼我們一點感覺都沒有？」).
6. E06–E07 paraphrase 「就像坐在大船的船艙裡，窗戶關上，船在走，你卻一點感覺也沒有」; the diagram carries the quotes “Like sitting in a big boat, windows shut” and “The boat moves, unfelt”.
7. E08 = 「從岸上看，船在動；從艙裡看，一切都靜止」. The diagram's small line under “From the shore” (「岸和水都在往後退」) is rendered “shore and water slip by”.
8. E09 “About 1,600 years later, in 1632, Galileo put people in a ship’s cabin too” = 「大約一千六百年後，伽利略在一六三二年……也把人關進大船的船艙」 (not “the identical cabin”, per the Chinese v2 fix).
9. E10 “a yearly back-and-forth versus Earth spinning and circling the Sun” = 「四游是大地一年來回平移，天照樣繞著地轉；伽利略講的，是地球自轉、又繞著太陽公轉」 (the turning sky is on the diagram line, not in the strip, for length).
10. E11 “Still, don’t say China found Earth’s motion first” = 「所以別說成『中國比伽利略更早發現地動』」 (“Earth’s motion” = 地動, so the sentence cannot be read as “China did not have the insight”).
11. Diagram dates: the timeline says “Han, around 1 CE” for 「西漢末～東漢初」 (the Chinese script: 約公元前後).
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 78 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
