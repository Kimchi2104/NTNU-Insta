## YouTube

- **Title**: Who drew the lines between the stars? (Ruelle, 1786)
- **Description**:
  The lines that join the stars in your sky app were never set by the International Astronomical Union (IAU): in the 1920s it defined only the borders of 88 constellations. So the same lion is drawn with 10 lines, 18 lines or 32 lines. In 1786 Alexandre Ruelle, a “student astronomer” at the Paris Observatory, took the pictures off his star map and kept only straight lines. Stars are astronomy; lines are culture.
  World Sky Cultures: the same sky, seen by different peoples. From the NTNU Astronomy Club, Taiwan. New episode every Wednesday.
  Sources: Stellarium sky cultures (ruelle, transcribed by Doina Bucur from A. Ruelle, Nouvelle Uranographie, 1786, BnF; western; western_rey); KBR and BnF catalogues; IAU 1922/1928.

Who drew the lines in your star app? Not the IAU (the International Astronomical Union). ✏️

## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the “last week in Sardinia, Leo was a sickle” bridge (the Sa Frache lines on page 2 are deleted); Ruelle’s age (30), the map’s width and its full title; the dedication to the young duc de Chartres (later King Louis-Philippe); the 70 → geometric figures count is kept only in the headline; the vanished constellations (Cerberus, Argo Navis, Musca Borealis) and the Bharaṇī link to A-28; H. A. Rey’s 1952 redraw (Rey stays only as the 18-line lion); the timeline diagram; the Dunhuang star chart; Doina Bucur’s digitisation and her study of 1,802 figures (“line figures have accents”); the Taipei 4/30 look-up card, Jupiter and the late Moon; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E03 “In the 1920s it defined 88 constellations — borders only, like countries on a map. Never the lines.” = 02 鏡「在一九二〇年代定下八十八個星座，規定的只是天空裡的『邊界』，像地圖上的國界；裡面怎麼連線，從來沒規定」.
2. E04–E06 counts 10 / 18 / 32 = Stellarium western / western_rey / ruelle, as in 03 鏡. “The star app Stellarium, by default” = 「星空軟體 Stellarium 預設的獅子」.
3. E05 “who drew Curious George” = 「畫《好奇猴喬治》的 H. A. Rey」 (the Chinese does not name Margret Rey on screen either).
4. E06 “student astronomer” in quotes, as the Chinese does (his own élève; BnF calls him professor 1783–1795: hedge kept by the quotes). Age 30 dropped.
5. E07 “Star maps then hid the stars inside mythical figures and animals” = 06 鏡「當時的星圖，星座大多畫成神話人物和動物，星星藏在圖畫裡」. “Most” carries 大多.
6. E08 = 07 鏡「鐮刀、身體、後腿，被切成一個個三角形和多邊形；連今天屬於小獅座的五顆星，也一起畫進大獅子裡」.
7. E09 “European star maps only slowly adopted lines in the 19th century” = v1 09 鏡「要到十九世紀，才慢慢改畫連線」. No “first” claim anywhere (the Chinese avoids it; Stellarium says earliest attempt in the Western tradition).
8. E11 (headline “Northern skies: …”) is written for about 40° N, early May, around 21:30: Leo’s Regulus ~49° up in the south-west, Denebola ~63° in the south (PyEphem, 2027-05-05). Mars on 5 May is still in Leo, ~3.8° from Regulus, mag ≈ +0.4 (Chinese 4/30: 5.1°, +0.3). The Mars dot on page 21 is drawn for 30 Apr; it is ~1.3° off by 5 May, invisible at this scale. Crater is left to the labels (it is only ~30° up from 40° N).
9. E12 “connect the stars your way” = 14 鏡「拿張紙，照你的眼睛連連看——你的線，也是一種星空文化」 (16 words / 7 s = 2.3).
10. E13 “Australia: an emu made of darkness” = EN27’s title; the Chinese 15 鏡 says 「那裡的人不只連星星，也連銀河裡的黑影」, which A-31 hedges — the English keeps only the emu.
11. The cover’s second line is the hook written for this v0 (*Who drew the lines in your star app?*), because the English schedule had none yet (「中文 v1 定稿後再定」); the schedule’s hook cell is filled with it.
12. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
