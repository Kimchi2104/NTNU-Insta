## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday’s Milky Way episode and the Qixi start of the Chinese series; Korea’s stone chart and banknote; Japan’s farm-tool star names (the Chinese 「農具」 in 12 鏡 is replaced by “a fishing net” in E09, see 審稿合併); the Inuit polar-night star clock; Māori Matariki; Arabia’s Pleiades and 28 lunar stations; India’s 27 wives of the Moon; Egypt’s Sirius and the Nile flood; al-Ṣūfī’s book; Ruelle’s 1786 line-only chart; the Navajo winter-story rule; the Tukano flood names; Romania’s shepherd and well; the Norse wolf’s mouth; the shared Bugis–Mandar stingray; the four world tours (Pleiades, Orion’s Belt, Big Dipper, Milky Way) and the three navigation episodes; the count (27 cultures + a dozen-plus guests; 47 episodes + 3 specials), the world map and the 27-cultures card; both corrections (Vega–Altair 14.6 light-years, not 16; the “toilet” asterism is south of Orion, not in the Purple Forbidden Enclosure); the Taipei Moon and moonset times; the Chinese season-two premiere on Qixi and Father’s Day, and “exactly one lunar year”; the Taipei Qixi card.

Wording to check:
1. E01 drops the number 27: the 27 counts cultures with their own episode in the **Chinese** season (A series), and the English series has covered a different, smaller set by 7/14. The schedule title already says “Many Ways of Seeing One Sky”.
2. E02 “China saw a dragon, a Forbidden City and a white tiger” = 02 鏡「在中國的天上，我們看見一條龍、一座紫禁城、一隻白虎」.
3. E03 “Mongolians tied the sky to a golden stake. An Evenki hunter chases a moose every night.” = 03 鏡「蒙古人把天空拴在一根金樁上；……埃文基獵人追著一頭駝鹿，每天晚上重演一次」 (the v1 correction: not “forever”).
4. E04 “sailing home by the stars, no instruments” = 04 鏡「夏威夷、東加、阿努塔的人不用儀器，靠星星回家」.
5. E05 “painted the Big Dipper and the Pleiades on tipi tops” = 06 鏡「黑腳族把北斗和昴宿，畫在帳篷頂上」 (v1 correction: 頂上, not just “on tipis”).
6. E06 “Belarusian farmers saw the Big Dipper as Elijah’s fiery chariot” = 07 鏡「白俄羅斯的農民，把北斗看成先知以利亞的火焰馬車」; “the prophet” is dropped for length.
7. E07 “link Milky Way darkness, not just stars” (present tense: the Kamilaroi and Euahlayi peoples are living cultures) = 08 鏡「不只連星星，也連銀河裡的黑暗」 (v1 correction: they link stars too, not “not stars”).
8. E08 “The same Big Dipper: a ship to the Bugis, a fishing net to the Mandar” = 08 鏡「同一個北斗，一邊看成船，一邊看成漁網」 (Bugis = ship, Mandar = net, as EN21 and the page 27 labels).
9. E09/E10 = 12 鏡; “almost every culture” keeps 「幾乎每個文化」. E09 says “a fishing net” where the Chinese says 農具 (see 審稿合併).
10. E11 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of 13 鏡 (Taipei 7/9: Moon sets 22:37, Summer Triangle high in the east, Galactic Centre due south). EN36 airs 7/14, when the Moon is past first quarter and sets after midnight (Chinese table: 7/14 01:09), so the Moon and the Milky Way centre are left out; the Summer Triangle is bright enough to see anyway. Degree labels removed from page 59.
11. E12 “Vega and Altair: the Weaver and the Cowherd. Their night, Qixi, falls on 8 August.” = 14 鏡「第二季，從今年七夕出發：八月八日」 (the Chinese verifies 2027 Qixi = 8/8). The Chinese season-two premiere is not mentioned; EN39 (Qixi) is scheduled for 8/8 but is not promised on screen.
12. E13 “Thank you for looking up with us.” = 15 鏡「謝謝你這一年，陪我們抬頭」 (“this year” dropped: the English series has not run a year).
13. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
14. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 86 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
