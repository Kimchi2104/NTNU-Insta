## YouTube

- **Title**: The emu made of darkness: a calendar in the Milky Way
- **Description**:
  Most constellations join bright stars. This one is made of the dark. For the Kamilaroi and Euahlayi peoples of northern New South Wales, the Coalsack beside the Southern Cross is an emu's head, and the dark lanes of the Milky Way down to Scorpius and Sagittarius are its neck, body and legs. Through the year its pose tells when emus run, when to collect eggs, when to stop, and when the waterholes are full.
  We share only what has been made public, mostly by Euahlayi Elder Ghillar Michael Anderson and the researchers he wrote with.
  World Sky Cultures: the same sky, seen by different peoples. From the NTNU Astronomy Club, Taiwan. New episode every Wednesday.
  Sources: Fuller, Anderson, Norris & Trudgett 2014, JAHH 17(2):171–179; Stellarium sky culture “kamilaroi” (R. S. Fuller; illustrations G. M. Anderson; CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

## Caption (Instagram / Facebook / Threads)

They join the stars — and the dark between. 🌌
For the Kamilaroi and Euahlayi peoples of northern New South Wales, the Coalsack beside the Southern Cross is the head of an emu, Gawarrgay. Its neck, body and legs are the dark lanes of the Milky Way, all the way to Scorpius and Sagittarius.
Its pose is a calendar: in April–May it runs; June–August it sits on the nest (time to collect eggs); in late August it stands upright as two eggs (stop — the chicks are about to hatch); in November it sits in the waterhole (the waterholes are full).
This month, after midnight, look south: from mid-northern latitudes the head never rises, but the body and legs do.
We share only what has been made public, mostly by Euahlayi Elder Ghillar Michael Anderson and the researchers he wrote with.
Next Wednesday: southern crosses — true, false and diamond.

## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week in Paris" bridge; the map and the 19th-century records (02 鏡, kept only as "peoples of northern New South Wales"); the bright-stars-vs-dark-lanes diagram (04 鏡's second half; the idea stays in E04's headline); the bora ceremonial grounds and the two circles (10 鏡); the Yarran tree and the two cockatoos Muraay (11 鏡); the same dark patch in other cultures: Boorong Tchingal, Bugis goat, Quechua Yutu and the black llama (12 鏡); the Guarani rhea Guirá Nhandu (13 鏡); the Taipei / Kenting altitudes and times and the "this week" card (14–15 鏡, replaced by the Northern-Hemisphere line); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 "peoples live in northern New South Wales" = 02 鏡「住在澳洲新南威爾斯北部」.
2. E03 headline "Only what they share publicly" + text = 02–03 鏡「主要來自尤瓦萊長老 Ghillar Michael Anderson 的公開分享，以及他跟研究者一起發表的論文」「還有很多知識只屬於族人；這裡只講他們願意公開的部分」. "Elder" capitalised as a title (Australian usage).
3. E04 headline "This emu is made of darkness" keeps the Chinese limit (說的是這隻鴯鶓，不是整個文化 — the Kamilaroi also have bright-star constellations); "dust clouds, hundreds of light-years away" = 「幾百光年外的塵埃雲」.
4. E05 "the dark patch beside the Southern Cross" = 「南十字旁邊這塊黑」; E06 "runs past Alpha Centauri and Hadar" = 「經過南門二和馬腹一」.
5. E08–E11 follow Stellarium's calendar as the Chinese does (06–09 鏡): April–May running (females chasing males); June–August on the nest (the Chinese adds 「真的鴯鶓，是爸爸負責孵蛋」 = E09); late August upright as two eggs, stop collecting; November in the waterhole; below the horizon = the black emu on Earth (Stellarium version; the paper's "waterholes dry" version is in the Chinese first comment only).
6. Diagram ③ subtitle reads "head + body = two eggs: chicks about to hatch — stop collecting" (Chinese: 「頭＝小蛋、身體＝大蛋：小鴯鶓快孵出來了，別撿」); the circle labels still say small egg (head) / big egg (body).
7. **E12–E13 (new wording, derived)**: the Chinese gives Taipei 2.6° / Kenting 5.5° for the head (Coalsack, dec −62.65°). Its highest altitude is about 90° − latitude − 62.65°, so it never rises north of about 28° N — "Farther north, it never rises". "This month, after midnight: look south" = the Chinese 「凌晨一點到三點……身體和腳爬到最高」 (Scorpius–Sagittarius culminate 01:00–02:40 in early May); for 35–45° N they stay low (Shaula about 8–18°). Please check the hedge "barely clears the horizon" against the picture (Kenting horizon, head about 4° at 23:00).
8. E15 "Southern crosses: true, false and diamond" = EN28's working title.
9. The cover's second line is the schedule's hook, a translation of the Chinese 節目表 hook 「他們不只連星星，也連銀河裡的黑」.
10. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 84 s).
