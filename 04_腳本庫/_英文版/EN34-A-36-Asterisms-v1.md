## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday (A-35, the 88 constellations; kept only as the fact “88 official constellations”); “ten-odd stars” in the Great Bear’s figure (in diagram C-A36-01 only: 19); the Great Square of Pegasus and Alpheratz (Shi and Bi in China); the Teapot = ostriches + the Southern Dipper; the Coathanger and diagram C-A36-03; retired constellations as asterisms (Mural Quadrant, Charles’ Oak, C-A36-04); the IAU Working Group on Star Names (2016–2017); Formosa and Sazum; the 18 June full Moon, the Teapot by the Moon and card C-A36-05; card C-A36-06; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (Matariki, which in English aired the week before).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “The sky is split into 88 official constellations. Many shapes you spot first aren’t on the list” = 01 鏡「天空被切成八十八塊；可是你最先認得的圖案，很多根本不在名單上」.
2. E03 “the Great Bear’s rear and tail” = 02 鏡「大熊座的後半身和尾巴」.
3. E04 headline “Famous, but unofficial: an asterism” = 「這種大家都認得、卻不是正式星座的圖案，叫星群」.
4. E05 = 03 鏡「在中國，北斗本來就是一個獨立的星官，管四季、定方向」 (present tense “is”, as the Chinese; “marking” for 管); headline “It depends whose rules” = 「什麼算星座，要看你用誰的規則」. “keeping the seasons and directions” paraphrases 《史記》「建四時」「臨制四鄉」 as the Chinese does. The Chinese “到了 IAU，它只是星群” is carried by E03/E04. Tone: not “China got it wrong” (the Chinese script’s ⚠).
5. E06 = 04 鏡; distances only in diagram C-A36-02 (25.0 / 16.7 / over 1,000 ly; Deneb estimates 1,400–2,600 ly as in the Chinese).
6. E08/E09 = 05 鏡; “each eagle has two small stars — an asterism of its own” = 「在阿拉伯，它們各帶兩顆小星，本身就是兩個星群」. “Vega comes from Arabic for ‘the swooping eagle’” — strictly, Vega comes from *al-wāqiʿ* “the swooping (one)”, part of the phrase; the Chinese also simplifies (考據備忘: 英文名各取原詞組的一部分).
7. E10/E11 are the Northern-Hemisphere version of 12 鏡 (v1 says so on screen: “Northern sky”) (Taipei, 18 June 22:00). For 30 June the same sky comes about 48 minutes earlier, so “late evening” still matches: Summer Triangle east, Big Dipper northwest with the handle up, Arcturus then Spica in the west (Chinese: 大角 260°, 角宿一 229°). The full Moon of 18 June is dropped; the Chinese notes moon-free early evenings after 26 June, so nothing on screen depends on the Moon.
8. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
9. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 80 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
