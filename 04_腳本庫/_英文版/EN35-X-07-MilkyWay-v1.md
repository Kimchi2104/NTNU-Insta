## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday’s Māori Matariki episode; the Book of Songs line 「維天有漢，監亦有光」; the roads in detail (Blackfoot Wolf Trail to the Spirit World, Guarani tapir path, Lokono clay carriers’ footprints) — kept only as “a road for souls”; the bird paths (Sámi, Russian goose road, Finnish Linnunrata) and the Bering Strait raven’s snowshoe track; the Mongolian seam and the Igloolik divider of winds (the seam kept only as one word); the Māori long fish and Tamarēreti’s canoe; the Mandar dragon’s fishing use and wind warning (kept only as “a dragon”); the ǀXam girl’s wood ash and Vanuatu Nemrau (kept only as “ash”); the Navajo name and the winter-only story rule; the English name and Hera’s milk (“相傳”); “hundreds of billions of stars” (still on the disc diagram); the Taipei viewing times, 36° and the C-X07-04 card; the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E01 “a river, a road, a seam” = 統一開頭「一條河、一條路、一道縫」; “The world saw” = 「全世界看見」.
2. E02 “Tianhe, the Heavenly River” = 02 鏡「中國人叫它天河、河漢」.
3. E03 “star pools, a boat, a ford, and another river” = 03 鏡「幾座水池，像咸池；還有一艘天船、一個渡口天津，和一條天江」; it does not say the whole river is water (the Chinese 考據 warns against that).
4. E04 “The ancient Erya: between Ji and Dou lies the river’s ford” = 04 鏡「《爾雅》說，箕斗之間，是銀河的渡口」 (Erya, Shitian: 箕、斗之間，漢津也).
5. E05 “Ji’s four stars were ostriches going to drink. Four in Dou are heading back, having drunk.” = 「箕宿這四顆星，在古阿拉伯是四隻鴕鳥，正走下河去喝水；斗宿裡的四顆，是喝飽了、正要離開的鴕鳥」 (φ, σ, τ, ζ Sgr; λ, μ are not ostriches — on the diagram). Wording follows the 譯名表 (A-19).
6. E06 “The ford lies between the flocks” = 「中國人的渡口，就在牠們中間」.
7. E07 “a road for souls, a seam, a dragon, even ash” compresses 05–09 鏡 (Blackfoot, Mongolian, Mandar, ǀXam/Vanuatu); each word is on the English names card on page 28.
8. E09 “a disc about 100,000 light-years wide” = 11 鏡「一個十萬光年寬的大盤子」 (the Chinese 考據: 直徑約 10 萬光年).
9. E11 “On summer nights we face the galaxy’s centre: in Sagittarius, right by that ford, 27,000 light-years away” = 12 鏡「夏夜，我們正對著盤子的中心，就在人馬座、剛才那個渡口旁邊，兩萬七千光年外」 (Sgr A* 4.6° from γ² Sgr).
10. E12 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of 12 鏡 (Taipei: 7/2–7/6, 21:00–01:00, due south at 23:00, 36° high): no times or dates, because EN35 airs 7/7 and the Chinese viewing window ends 7/6–7/7 (7/7 the Moon sets 21:25 in Taipei). “low in the south” because the centre culminates at 90° − latitude − 29° (21° at 40° N); “far from city lights” = 「找個遠離城市燈光、南方開闊的地方」.
11. The cover’s second line is the new English hook (also written into the schedule), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
12. The working title said “a Rift” (schedule); on screen and on YouTube the word is “seam”, which is what the Chinese 縫線 means. v1 renames the title here and in the schedule (and the EN34 next-episode card).
13. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 86 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.

2.
