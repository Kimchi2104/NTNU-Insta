## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the A-22 bridge 「昨天白俄羅斯那集」 (EN16 comes four days *after* EN15, so the link moves to the end card); 「十五的月亮十六圓」 and the full-Moon time (2/21 07:23 Taipei = 2/20 23:23 UTC is still "tonight" in the Americas, so "tomorrow morning" would be wrong for part of the audience); the Taipei times 「晚上七到十點」 and the exact layout 「月亮上方是木星、左下是火星」 (holds in Taipei 19:00–22:00; the Moon moves about 0.5° an hour, so by evening in Europe or America it has slid east and is no longer strictly "between" them — v0 says "close by"); the pointer back to A-02 《三垣》 and the Romania teaser (not in English yet); everything in the first comment (the Three Officials' origin in the Celestial Masters' "three letters", the Four Sovereigns, which wall stars set in Taipei, the penumbral lunar eclipse).

Wording to check:
1. Cover "On Lantern Festival night — people honour the Heaven Official." = 「元宵這一晚——人們拜天官」. 拜 → "honour" (not "pray to"/"worship", to stay neutral).
2. E02 "It’s also the Upper Yuan Festival: the Heaven Official’s birthday." = 02 鏡「元宵是上元節，天官大帝的生日」. The Chinese voice-over says it flatly; the first comment adds that the "birthday" is folk usage (early Daoist texts call it the day the Three Officials review merit). v0 keeps the voice-over wording.
3. E04 "He’s also called Ziwei Dadi" = 「天官又稱紫微大帝」 (又稱 = also called; never "is"). "the northern sky’s centre" = 「北天正中央那片星區」.
4. E06 "One is a god, the other a star region" = 04 鏡「一個是信仰裡的神，一個是古人分的星區」; "the Sky Emperor’s palace, pivot of the sky" = 「紫微垣是天帝的宮城，整片天繞著它轉」. The Chinese card 「同名，兩回事」 → E05 headline "Same name, two things".
5. E07 "Sima Qian called it the Sky Emperor’s chariot" = 「司馬遷說『斗為帝車』」 (帝 = 天帝, as the Chinese card 「北斗＝天帝的車」).
6. E08 is the Northern-Hemisphere version of 06 鏡: no clock time; "nearly full" = 99.7 % at 20:00 Taipei; Moon–Jupiter 5.3°, Moon–Mars 6.7°. "close by" is true all evening across the Northern Hemisphere on 2/20.
7. Diagram C-HYX-01 (English) splits "Upper Yuan" and "1st month, 15th day" onto two lines and shrinks "Heaven Official grants blessings" so it fits the box; C-HYX-02 keeps "a.k.a. Ziwei Dadi" (又稱) and "Book of Jin: seat of the Great Emperor".
8. E09: EN16 (A-22 Belarus) publishes Wednesday 2/24, so "This Wednesday".
