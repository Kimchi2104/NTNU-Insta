## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the "last week on Anuta" bridge; the magnitudes 4.3 / 5.1 (kept only on the page's own labels); the Kamilaroi shy sister and the Blackfoot six lost children; the three chase stories (Kamilaroi boys, Nama hunter and zebras, Arabic al-Dabaran); naming each Pacific island in the text (left to the labels and card); Macedonia, Belarus and China's 昴日雞 hens (labels only); the Ticuna turtles, Sardinia, the Aztec market (labels only); "three to four thousand years ago" for Babylon; Lokono and Zulu calendar uses; the dusk rising in November, Makahiki, Tahiti's season of plenty and Hesiod; the cluster's real size, age and the distance dispute; the Subaru car and telescope; the Taipei weekend viewing guide, moonrise times and the lunar occultation; the Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday's English episode).

Wording to check:
1. Cover: "The whole world saw — one tiny cluster" = 「同一片星空，全世界都看見——這一小團星」. The cover's second line is the schedule's hook (*Japan calls it Subaru. The Māori call it Matariki.*), not a translation.
2. E04 "hard to see from cities" = 「第七亮只有五點一等，城市裡幾乎看不到」.
3. E05 "From New Zealand to Hawaiʻi, its name is almost the same word" = 「到了太平洋，它幾乎是同一個字」 (the Chinese lists Māori/Anuta, Tahiti, Samoa, Tonga, Hawaiʻi; the labels on the page still do).
4. E07 keeps the Chinese balance: linguistics → "little eyes"; Māori lore → a god's eyes (fact-check item 5 of the Chinese v1.1).
5. E10 "a word for gathering things into one bundle" = 「來自『統ばる』——把散的東西束成一把」.
6. E11 Sima Qian "Mao is the 'hairy head'" = 「昴曰髦頭，髦，是長長的頭髮」; "Indonesia's Bugis" = 「印尼的布吉斯人」. E11 is the densest beat (16 words, 7 s, 2.3 w/s).
7. E13 "Each June it returns before dawn … Matariki, the Māori New Year" = 「每年六月，它在黎明前重新出現：紐西蘭把這叫 Matariki 新年」; the public-holiday detail is only in the first comment.
8. E14 is the one rewritten line (Northern-Hemisphere mid-latitudes, early December, not Taipei): "up all night" follows the Chinese 星空對時 (after the dusk rising in mid-November it is visible all night); "by midnight it rides high in the south" replaces 「半夜十一點多，它會經過頭頂」 (overhead at Taipei; at 35–50° N it culminates 64–79° up in the south around 23:00–midnight in early December). Please check this is acceptable.
9. Glossary: the X-01 script spells **al-Thurayya** and the shared glossary has **al-Thurayyā** (A-06, A-13). al-Thurayya only appears on the English card, written as in the Chinese script.
