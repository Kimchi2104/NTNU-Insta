## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the bridge from last Friday’s India episode; the map and sheep/people numbers diagram (C-A29-01; only “about two to one” is kept); the three collectors Bussu, Puddu and Putzolu and the year 2017 (kept in the sources); Su Gurdone, the Barbagia variant; “the same as in Spain and Latin America” for the Three Marias; Canis Major “the driver of the sticks” and Sirius, “the steel star”; Venus, “the supper star”, and the morning star (C-A29-07); the Greek she-goat and kids reduced to “a goat”; the Seven Brothers (Big Dipper); the cross of St Constantine and the S’Ardia horse race (C-A29-03); the widows’ dance and the Romanian Hora; the deer horn and Sardinian red deer; the straw road (C-A29-04); the 2026 call for help and the name dictionary card (C-A29-05); the Taipei weekend look-up card (C-A29-06); the next-Friday Chinese teaser (replaced by next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 = 02 鏡「薩丁尼亞在義大利西邊，是地中海第二大島；到今天，島上的羊大約是人的兩倍」; “sheep outnumber people about two to one” = 「大約是人的兩倍」 (ISTAT 2024: ~3.07 M sheep, ~1.57 M people).
2. E03 = 02 鏡「這套星座，用的是島中部小鎮 Sedilo 的方言」.
3. E04 = 04 鏡「Bussu 的書，把這些星名讀成島上的生活：牧人的小屋、葡萄農的一串葡萄、農夫的鐮刀」; “One book” keeps the attribution to Bussu’s reading without naming him on screen.
4. E05 = 05 鏡「金牛座的 V 字，連上畢宿五，叫 Su Pinnetu：牧人用石頭疊起圓牆、再搭上木枝尖頂的小屋」.
5. E06 = 06 鏡「昴宿，叫 S'Udrone：一大串葡萄……羅馬尼亞人在這裡看見母雞帶小雞，薩丁尼亞結的是葡萄」.
6. E07 = 07 鏡「獵戶座叫 Sos Bacheddos：一根根牧杖；腰帶三顆另有名字：Sas Tres Marias，三個瑪利亞」; 牧杖 = “the shepherds’ sticks” (Stellarium: The Sticks).
7. E08 and the three diagram headlines = 08 鏡「這幾根牧杖，是牧人的夜間時鐘。夏天，羊群在夜裡吃草；後半夜，先看到那串葡萄爬上來，接著 Sos Bacheddos 露臉，就該把羊趕回圈了」; “until the sticks rise” compresses the grapes-then-sticks sequence, which the diagram headlines (E08a–c) show step by step.
8. E09 = 11 鏡 (“Up north” dropped) 「御夫座叫 Sa Mandra，羊圈。希臘人在這裡看見抱著母山羊和小羊的御夫；薩丁尼亞人看見的，是圈羊的柵欄」; “with a goat” drops the kids.
9. E10 = 12 鏡「獅子座，那把反寫問號的鐮刀，叫 Sa Frache，就是農人的記號」; “the farmer’s sickle” follows the label table (The Sickle) and 04 鏡「農夫的鐮刀」. “In northern skies this week, look high in the southwest after dark” replaces the Taipei tip (19 鏡; not in the Chinese script). Taurus and Orion have set by then, so the hut and sticks are not in the tip.
10. English diagram C-A29-02: the existing English strings in `make_a29_diagrams.py`; “S'Udrone (Pleiades) rises” shortened to “S'Udrone rises” so it does not run into “Sos Bacheddos appear (Belt rises)” when the layers stack.
11. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook (written for this v0 from the opening line: “The stars of Taurus? Sardinian shepherds saw a stone hut.”).
