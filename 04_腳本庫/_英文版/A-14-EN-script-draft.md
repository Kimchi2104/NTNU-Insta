## Section 5: Cut & Semantic Adjustment Log

1. **Opening Hook**: Applied standard opening formula: *"One sky. The Inuit of the polar night saw — a clock of stars."*
2. **Polar Night Duration Accuracy**: Corrected the geographical polar night duration to approximately six weeks (46 days in Igloolik at 69.4°N), avoiding exaggerated claims of "two full months" (which only occurs above 71°N).
3. **Indigenous Terminology and Provenance**: Strictly adopted authentic Igloolik Inuktitut orthography from elder oral accounts collected in John MacDonald's *The Arctic Sky*: *Nuutuittuq*, *Tukturjuit*, *Quturjuuk*, *Pituaq*, *Ursuutaattiaq*, *Aagjuuk*, *Ullaktut*, *Nanurjuk*, *Qimmiit*, *Singuuriq*, and *qauppat*.
