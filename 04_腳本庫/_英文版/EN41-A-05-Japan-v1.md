## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode): the Subaru-on-the-car opening (01, moved later as E08); the whole Kitora tomb story (02–05: discovery 1983/1998, 360+ gold-leaf stars, 74 asterisms, the four circles, “oldest of its kind” wording, the latitude/date dispute); the Korea comparison (08); the southern mansions sweep (09: Chichiri … Mitsukake); the Hyades folk names and the *Shijing* rain line (11); the *Kojiki* and *Makura no Sōshi* citations and the dictionary’s title on screen (12); the company president’s name and the dialect diagram (13); Shikoku’s Kaseboshi (yarn reel) and the other belt names (14); Myōken, the Pole Star cult and the Onmyōryō secret reports (16); the September Taipei rising times and the next-Friday Chinese teaser (17, replaced by a January Northern-Hemisphere tip and next Wednesday’s English episode).

Wording to check:
1. E02 “the whole grid, but not the names” = 06 鏡「照抄了整套格子，卻沒有照抄名字」.
2. E03 “In the Edo period, scholars gave each mansion a Japanese reading” = 07 鏡「江戶時代，學者替二十八宿每一宿配了一個日文讀法」.
3. E04 “a rice-levelling rod” = 「刮平米斗的那根棒子」; “possibly a bellows” keeps the hedge 「辭書說是踩著送風的煉鐵風箱」 (和名表 △ 推測). “Foot-” and “iron-smelting” dropped so the text fits 3 lines.
4. E05 “mid-Edo scholars’ readings, not old folk star names … 11 of the 28 still have no agreed meaning” = 10 鏡.
5. E06 “A dictionary recorded it around 934 CE” = 12 鏡「《和名類聚抄》——大約九三四年——寫『昴星，和名須八流』」. The Chinese 01 says 「日本現存最古老的星名」; the English uses the safer “one of Japan’s oldest” only in the caption (考據 calls it 「最早的星名記錄之一」).
6. E07 “to gather scattered things into one bundle” = 「統ばる：把散的東西束成一把」 (stated without a hedge in the Chinese; Nojiri calls it the settled view).
7. E08 “Six stars, six companies” compresses 13 鏡「六顆星，是五家出資公司加上富士重工」. The brand is named only in the caption; no logo is drawn.
8. E09 “often with the three small stars below” = 考據「腰帶三顆，多半還要算上下面的小三星」 (the dictionary gives the belt only).
9. E10 names exactly as the label table (Stellarium): Belarus Kastsy = mowers (「割草的人」), Siberia Kichigi = threshers, Tukano Sioyahpu = adze handle. “of Sulawesi” and “Amazon” moved onto the labels so the text fits 3 lines.
10. E11 “Unlikely to be borrowed” = 15 鏡「這不太可能是互相抄來的」 (a hedge, kept).
11. E12 is the only rewritten fact: the Chinese tip is for Taipei in mid-September (rising order Pleiades → Hyades → Orion after 21:00). For 17 January at Northern mid-latitudes the Pleiades cross the meridian around 20:00 local time and Orion’s Belt (RA 5h36m) about 1¾ h later, so “high in the south … Orion’s Belt follows to the southeast” holds.
12. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook (“In Japan, Orion’s Belt is a plough.”, set in this card), not a translation of the Chinese cover.
13. Page sequence is the authority for timing (total 89 s). The Sec column in Beats is not additive.
