## Cuts and changes (for the reviewer)

Dropped from the Chinese version (all still in the Chinese episode or its fact-check notes): the "forced to work on Valentine’s Day" joke; "the Weaver west of the river, the Cowherd east" (correct for the real sky, but the traditional story says the opposite — fact-check ⚠, so left out rather than explained); the premiere teaser "in two days: a dragon across the eastern sky" (out of date, and the fact-check notes the dragon is in the south-west); the Caption’s "east-south-east" sky tip. The aired Canva has much more (Deneb 1,400–2,600 ly and 5,000× Vega; Hegu as an army drum; Tianjin and the imperial road; the farm and weaving asterisms; who Qianniu was; other peoples’ names for these stars; Vega as a past and future pole star) — none of it is in the repo’s Chinese v1 script, so none is used here.

Wording to check:
1. E01 / E06 "14.6 years" = fact-check correction (Chinese v1 says 16): Hipparcos 2007, Vega 25.04 ly, Altair 16.73 ly, angle 34.20° → 14.6 ly. The aired Canva page 11 already shows 14.6.
2. E03 “Northern Hemisphere, August evenings — the brightest star nearly overhead” = Chinese 「天頂附近最亮的那顆就是織女星」, with the fact-check’s direction correction: no compass direction (the Chinese "south-east" is wrong). For Northern mid-latitudes Vega passes within a few degrees of the zenith on August evenings; the qualifier is added because Vega (dec. +38.8°) is never overhead in the Southern Hemisphere.
3. E02 "Legend says: his carrying pole, with their two children" = 「傳說是他挑著扁擔，擔著一雙兒女」 (fact-check: the folk tale is attested).
4. E04 "in dark skies" is the fact-check’s note (the Milky Way needs a dark, moonless sky), not new content.
5. E07 "Often said to be Deneb" = 「搭起鵲橋的，是天鵝座的天津四」 with a hedge added: the fact-check notes the magpie bridge = Deneb is a modern popular telling; the ancient texts make the whole Tianjin asterism the ford across the river. "Heavenly Ford" = 「天津，就是天上的渡口」.
6. E08 is the closing card, not a translation of a script line (the aired Canva’s 同一片星空／不同的文化寫下了不同的故事／七夕快樂).
7. The cover’s second line is the schedule’s hook, not a translation of the Chinese cover.
8. No "Next Wednesday" card: the following Wednesday (2027-08-11) has no English episode; the next is EN46 on 2027-09-08.
