## 三、沒把握的地方
- **Cover hook decision (E01 vs. Page sequence p.1)**: Cuts & changes #8 mentions that `*The star shapes you know first? Many aren’t constellations.*` was already written into the publish schedule (`節目表_萬國星空_英文版.csv`). If the cover line must match the schedule rather than the unified opening (`One sky. ...`), then E01 should be updated to match p.1 instead — and E02 should be reworded so it doesn't repeat `shapes you ... first` back-to-back.
- **Hemisphere qualifier in E10**: If other English episodes in this series deliberately omit `Northern sky` in the stargazing beat and assume Northern Hemisphere mid-latitudes by default, the E10 edit can stay optional.
- **Period placement with double quotes (E08, E09)**: v0 follows British-style logical punctuation (`“the flying eagle”.`, `“the hen’s tail”.`) and omits the Oxford comma (`Vega, Altair and Deneb`). Both are internally consistent across the script and Caption; keep them as is unless the series house style standardizes on American punctuation (`“the flying eagle.”` + Oxford comma).
- **Fact-check pass**: All numbers (88 constellations; 25 / 17 / 1,000+ ly; 25.0 / 16.7 in diagram), star/constellation names, Arabic etymologies (`al-nasr al-wāqiʿ`, `al-nasr al-ṭāʾir`, `dhanab al-dajāja` in Glossary), and sky positions for late June (shifted ~48 min earlier from 6/18 22:00) align accurately with `A-36-現代星座下課本沒教的星群Asterisms-逐字稿-v1.md`.
