## 三、沒把握的地方

1. **Cover line vs. series opening line (E01)**: The header table says `Opening line: One sky. Astronomers cut it — into 88 pieces.`, while Page sequence page 1 says the cover's second line is `In 1930, the whole sky was carved into 88 pieces.`. I chose the 1930 hook in the revised Beats table because it stops scrollers much better, but please confirm whether the Canva cover template keeps both lines or only one.
2. **88 pieces vs. 89 pieces (E01 / Caption)**: Because Serpens's split into Caput and Cauda (making 89 regions for 88 constellations) was cut from the English version, saying "88 pieces" on the cover/E01 is completely consistent with the English script itself and matches the Chinese schedule hook (`把天空切成 88 塊`), though technically astronomers know it's 89 regions. Leaving "88 pieces" is fine for a 90-second overview, just flagging it in case you prefer `88 constellations` on the cover.
3. **Plancius without context (E05)**: An international viewer who doesn't know astronomy history may wonder who "Plancius" is (since the Chinese script also doesn't state his profession, I did not add "cartographer"). Keeping just `Plancius` is faithful to the Chinese script, though `Plancius turned them into 12 constellations` reads slightly more connected to E04's Dutch navigators.
