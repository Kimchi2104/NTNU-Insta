## 三、沒把握的地方

1. **Cover line choice (E01 vs Page sequence p.1)**: Beats E01 uses the series opening (`One sky. The ancient Arabs saw — a desert zoo.`), while Page sequence p.1 specifies the schedule’s hook (`Ostriches, gazelles, hyenas — a desert zoo overhead.`). Because I don’t know whether the English series requires keeping the `One sky.` formula on page 1, I kept E01 unchanged in Section 二 and flagged the mismatch in Section 一 so the merge pass can pick one consistently.
2. **Glossary note for Talitha**: Glossary line 74 lists `Talitha (ι κ UMa)`, which matches the Chinese 星名表 (where the pair is ι and κ UMa), though the Chinese 考據備忘 and 首留言 note that the IAU officially assigned `Talitha` only to `ι UMa`. Not on screen, so low priority, but worth double-checking if Glossary is reused later.
3. **Total runtime (84 s vs 87 s)**: The `Sec to next` column in Page sequence sums to 84 s up to the start of page 48 (E16, which is listed as 3 s in Beats). Worth confirming whether page 48’s 3 s is included in the 84 s total or makes the final export 87 s.
