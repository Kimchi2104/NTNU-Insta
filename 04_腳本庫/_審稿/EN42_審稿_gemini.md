## 三、沒把握的地方
- **E08 (`Water inside and out`) vs. p30 diagram**: The Chinese text says 「天表裡有水」 ("water outside and inside"), but if the p30 diagram (`天球_載水而浮層`) only illustrates water in the lower half *inside* the sphere, `Water inside and out` might look slightly mismatched against the visual. Please check p30 before deciding between `Water inside and out` and `Water inside the shell`.
- **E16 (`Tupi-Guarani`)**: Please verify whether the English glossary / next episode uses accents (`Tupí-Guaraní`) or plain ASCII (`Tupi-Guarani`), and confirm the year `1614` against that episode's fact-checked Chinese script (it is not in C-01 v2).
- **Diagram text**: This review only checked the on-screen Beats text in `EN42-C-01-ThreeTheories-v0.md`, not the baked-in English text inside `05_素材/C-01_論天三家/_概念圖/en/` PNG layers (e.g., whether `Xi Meng`, `Cai Yong`, and `Huntian Yi Zhu` are spelled consistently there).
