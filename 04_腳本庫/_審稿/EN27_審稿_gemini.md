## 三、沒把握的地方

1. **Diagram C-A31-03 ④ (page 17 / E11)**: I did not inspect the English PNG layers directly. Please verify that diagram layer ④ explicitly states **“waterholes are full”** (matching Chinese 09 鏡「代表水坑都滿了」and the English YouTube/Caption), since E11’s headline only covers the December “black emu” half and the Beat note only mentions `④ November: in the waterhole; December: below the horizon`.
2. **High northern latitudes in E13**: E13 says `Its body and legs — Scorpius to Sagittarius — climb into view.` For mid-northern latitudes (~30°–45° N, e.g., US, southern/central Europe, Japan), this is true in May after midnight, though very far north (e.g., >55° N, UK/Scandinavia/Canada), southern Scorpius (Shaula, dec −37°) stays below the horizon. The Caption wisely specifies “from mid-northern latitudes”; worth confirming whether the on-screen E13 wording is fine as a general statement or should stay paired with E12’s Taiwan/farther-north contrast.
3. **E01 Cover layout**: Unclear whether the English series template puts BOTH the schedule hook (`They joined the stars — and the dark between.`) AND the E01 line (`One sky. The Kamilaroi saw — an emu made of darkness.`) on page 1, or if one replaces the other. If both appear on a 3-second cover, it exceeds comfortable reading speed.
