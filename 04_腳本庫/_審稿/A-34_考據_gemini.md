## 三、可以加進影片的好材料（選填）

1. **歐洲抄本的「盲抄奇蹟」——西方畫師看不懂阿拉伯文，卻把 M31 畫了幾百年！**
   - 西西里與歐洲在中世紀流傳的拉丁文蘇菲手稿（*Sufi Latinus*，已知存世 8 部抄本），歐洲學者只翻譯了托勒密的星表數據，卻完全沒有翻譯蘇菲用阿拉伯文描述本土沙漠星官的長篇文字。
   - 然而，歐洲抄本的插畫師看著阿拉伯底本裡的仙女座圖，發現大魚嘴邊圍著一圈奇特的小黑點，雖然完全不知道是什麼意思，卻畢恭畢敬地把這群代表仙女座大星系（M31）的小黑點原樣照抄了幾百年！直到 1612 年西門·馬呂斯（Simon Marius）用望遠鏡觀測 M31，歐洲天文界才恍然大悟那個小點是什麼。
   - **出處**：Paul Kunitzsch (1987), "A Medieval Reference to the Andromeda Nebula", *The Messenger* (ESO), No. 49, pp. 42–43.

2. **蘇菲還是「衣架星團（Cr 399）」與「船帆座雙星團（IC 2391）」的真正發現者！**
   - 現代天文史研究證實，蘇菲的手稿不僅是人類最早記錄仙女座星系（M31）的文獻，他還在天鷹座與狐狸座交界處首次記錄了著名的「衣架星團」（Collinder 399 / Brocchi's Cluster，他在書中形容為天箭座附近的一只小碗，比歐洲天文學家 Hodierna 1654 年的獨立再發現早了近 700 年！）；同時他還在南天記錄了船帆座疏散星團 IC 2391（比法國天文學家拉卡伊 1752 年早了近 800 年！）。
   - **出處**：Ihsan Hafez (2010), *Abd al-Rahman al-Sufi and His Book of the Fixed Stars*, pp. 308–310, Table 16.

3. **阿拉伯星空裡還有「另一條魚」——咬著英仙座著名的雙星團！**
   - 蘇菲在仙女座章節記錄，阿拉伯沙漠除了仙女座身上橫跨的大魚（al-Ḥūt al-ʿAẓīm）之外，在北邊「大駱駝（仙后座）」肚子底下還游著「另一條魚（al-Samaka al-Ukhrā）」：這條魚的魚嘴在仙女座膝蓋旁，而魚尾巴正好咬在英仙座右手的雲斑上——那正是著名的**英仙座雙星團（h+χ Persei / NGC 869 & 884）**！阿拉伯人把 M31 當成大魚吐出的氣泡，把雙星團當成另一條魚的尾鰭。
   - **出處**：Al-Sufi, *Kitāb Ṣuwar al-Kawākib*, Andromeda Chapter；Schjellerup (1874), p. 119.

4. **蘇菲的「三級細分星等」領先西方數百年！**
   - 托勒密在《天文學大成》中只使用 1 到 6 等星，偶爾加註「偏亮」或「偏暗」；蘇菲則建立了極為嚴謹的「三級細分星等系統」：他在整數星等後使用 `Akbareh`（較大，相當於 $-0.25$ 等）、`Aṣghareh`（較小，相當於 $+0.25$ 等）與 `Aʿẓameh`（顯著較大，相當於 $-0.50$ 等），將整片星空的一千多顆星重新觀測分級，其精準度與現代視星等偏差的標準差遠小於托勒密星表。
   - **出處**：Ihsan Hafez (2010), Chapter 5.4, pp. 150–180, p. 349.

Arabic manuscript tradition: Oxford Bodleian Library MS. Marsh 144; Paris Bibliothèque nationale de France MS. Arabe 5036; Wikisource Arabic edition based on Library of Congress manuscript.
2. **Schjellerup, Hans Carl Frederik Christian (1874)**. *Description des étoiles fixes composée au milieu du dixième siècle de notre ère par l'astronome persan Abd-al-Rahman al-Sûfi*. Traduction littérale avec notes, Imprimerie de l'Académie Impériale des Sciences, Saint-Pétersbourg. [Andromeda chapter: pp. 118–119; Auriga: p. 83; Aquila: p. 106].
3. **Kunitzsch, Paul (1987)**. "A Medieval Reference to the Andromeda Nebula", *The Messenger* (European Southern Observatory), Issue 49, September 1987, pp. 42–43. Bibcode: `1987Msngr..49...42K`.
4. **Kunitzsch, Paul (1986)**. "The Astronomer Abu 'l-Ḥusayn al-Ṣūfī and his Book on the Constellations", *Zeitschrift für Geschichte der Arabisch-Islamischen Wissenschaften*, 3, pp. 56–81. [Reprinted in: *The Arabs and the Stars*, Variorum Reprints, Northampton, 1989, Article XI].
5. **Kunitzsch, Paul (1961)**. *Untersuchungen zur Sternnomenklatur der Araber*. Otto Harrassowitz, Wiesbaden. [al-Simāk: nos. 288–289].
6. **Kunitzsch, Paul & Smart, Tim (2006)**. *A Dictionary of Modern Star Names: A Short Guide to 254 Star Names and Their Derivations*. Sky Publishing, Cambridge, MA.
7. **Hafez, Ihsan (2010)**. *‘Abd al-Raḥmān al-Ṣūfī and His Book of the Fixed Stars: A Journey of Re-discovery*. Ph.D. Thesis, Centre for Astronomy, James Cook University, Townsville, Australia. 406 pp. [ResearchOnline@JCU Item 28854].
8. **Savage-Smith, Emilie (2013)**. "The Most Authoritative Copy of 'Abd al-Rahman al-Sufi's Tenth-Century Guide to the Constellations", in Philippe Foret & Kapila Vatsyayan (eds.), *The Journey of Maps and Images on the Silk Road*, Brill, Leiden, pp. 125–155.
9. **Wellesz, Emmy (1959)**. "An Early al-Ṣūfī Manuscript in the Bodleian Library in Oxford: A Study in Islamic Constellation Images", *Ars Orientalis*, Freer Gallery of Art / University of Michigan, Vol. 3, pp. 1–26, Plates 1–28.
10. **Lane, Edward William (1863–1893)**. *An Arabic-English Lexicon*. Williams and Norgate, London. Book I, Part 4, p. 1428 (Root **س م ك**).
11. **McConnachie, A. W. et al. (2005)**. "Distances and metallicities for 17 Local Group galaxies", *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*, 356 (4), pp. 979–997.
12. **Riess, Adam G. et al. (2012)**. "Milky Way Cepheid Standards for Measuring Cosmic Distances and Application to Gaia Photometry", *The Astrophysical Journal*, 745 (2), 156.
13. **Dürer, Albrecht, Stabius, Johannes & Heinfogel, Conrad (1515)**. *Imagines coeli Septentrionales cum duodecim imaginibus zodiaci*. Woodcut, Nuremberg. High-resolution scan: National Gallery of Art, Washington D.C., Accession 1943.3.3591 (Rosenwald Collection, CC0 Open Access); The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Accession 50.598.1.
14. **Stellarium Sky Culture: Arabic (Al-Sufi)**. Research & Arabic critical edition by Khalid al-Ajaji (2020); illustrations adapted by Kutaibaa Akraa from BnF Arabe 5036.
15. **Astronomical Ephemeris & Code**: PyEphem 4.2.1, Skyfield 1.55, IAU WGSN Star Names Catalog, IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature, NASA JPL Small-Body Database.
