The Bugis are an ethnic group on the island of Sulawesi in Indonesia, with a strong seafaring and trading tradition. This sky culture has
12
constellation line figures. It also has names for four stars and five other sky objects (the Pleiades, the Coalsack Nebula, the Magellanic Cloud(s), the Milky Way, and Venus). Many of the constellations reflect the seafaring tradition; the others are of agricultural origin.
